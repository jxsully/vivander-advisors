The Cheese Has Moved. A New Reality for Digital Marketing

Michael J. Goldrich · August 26, 2025

Your instincts may be telling you that something is off. The metrics aren’t adding up like they used to. Your best-performing content isn’t driving the same results. The ROI you could once measure with confidence (7:1, 10:1, even 15:1) now feels fuzzy, flat, or artificially inflated.

You’re not imagining it.

The rules of digital visibility have fundamentally changed.

To understand this shift, consider the popular business parable Who Moved My Cheese? In it, two mice and two tiny humans live in a maze, happily feeding on a dependable block of cheese until one day, the cheese is gone. The mice immediately begin searching for a new source. The humans, however, resist. They waste time analyzing, blaming, and hoping the cheese will come back.

But it doesn’t.

The lesson is simple: the cheese moved. You can either adapt or go hungry.

In our industry, the “cheese” was once clear: clicks, traffic, conversions, attribution. That cheese sat comfortably at the end of a linear funnel. You could follow it, fund it, and forecast it.

But the cheese has moved.

Today’s travelers are guided by AI platforms like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and others that summarize your brand, recommend you to guests, and make booking decisions often before a click ever happens. Visibility now precedes traffic. Influence happens off-site. Bookings are often misattributed. If you’re still only measuring what you can see (what gets clicked), you’re missing the majority of what matters.

What will pull us through this transformation is not fear or nostalgia. It’s a spirit of experimentation: a willingness to test, pilot, and learn. It’s a commitment to follow the data, not gut instinct, because the old signals no longer apply. It’s finding comfort in being uncomfortable, because the systems, platforms, and rules that once brought clarity now reward those who embrace complexity.

It’s about moving forward, systematically, together.

And yes, the cheese will keep moving.
