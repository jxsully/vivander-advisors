Real audits. Identifying details withheld.

The hotel case reports September 2026 observations; the restaurant and luxury retail cases report August 2026 observations. Names, locations and identifying competitor details are withheld. Conceptual artwork does not depict the audited businesses.

Baselines, not promised business outcomes.

Each case explains the counting rules, review limitations and pending re-tests. Answer observations do not prove lost sales or recovered revenue. The correction plans show what to check next.

What should your organization look for in the cases?

  1. The customer’s question. Does the test reflect a real decision, rather than a search for the brand’s own name?
  2. The answer evidence. Separate being mentioned, being cited and being recommended. Check the counting rules and repeated runs.
  3. The correction plan. Identify the fact or source to change, the person responsible and the re-test question.
  4. The limit of the claim. AI answer observations do not measure lost sales, prove recovered revenue or guarantee a future recommendation.
