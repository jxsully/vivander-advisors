Questions about working with Vivander

What should I check before using AI-generated content?

Check the source, factual claims, permissions, relevance and the people or situations represented. A convincing demonstration is not proof of accuracy. Use a defined human review step before publishing or acting.

Can Vivander help my team learn to review AI outputs?

Vivander training connects approved tools to real tasks and review routines. Share the task and audience you have in mind so the learning scope can be discussed.
