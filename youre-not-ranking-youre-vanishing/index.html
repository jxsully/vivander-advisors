You’re Not Ranking. You’re Vanishing.

Michael J. Goldrich · December 5, 2025

Phocuswright’s latest report says the quiet part out loud: search is slipping.

Generative AI is surging.

Travelers are turning to AI as their primary planning engine.

Forty percent of US travelers used generative AI to plan a trip this year.

That’s an 11-point jump.

This is behavioral rewiring.

These travelers are younger.

Wealthier.

Taking more international trips.

Spending more.

And making decisions directly from AI-generated answers.

Millennials lead the charge.

Gen Z is next.

Not because they’re late adopters.

Because they’ve built their own layer of AI discovery through social.

The handoff to generative tools is only a matter of time.

And the punchline?

AI is now just as trusted as travel publications.

It’s pulling even with supplier content.

The playing field is being rewritten.

The Invisible Internet Has Arrived

This is the era of zero-click travel.

Guests are not browsing.

They are prompting.

They are trusting the summary.

When AI-to-AI becomes the default transaction layer, discovery will be a private conversation between algorithms.

No links.

No loading.

No loyalty.

For Hotel Commercial Teams, This Changes Everything

Your website must act like an API, not a brochure.

It must contain structured data, entity signals, and clear schema.

Your story must be readable by models, not just guests.

Your team needs more than AI tools.

You need AI fluency.

AI literacy.

AI confidence.

You need content that doesn’t just persuade people.

It must train machines.

And you need it now.

The Direct Channel Is the Only Playable Card

OTAs will win this new game by default.

Their data is structured.

Indexed.

Optimized for algorithmic digestion.

Hotels must counter by converting their direct channel into a verified, structured source of truth.

You must collect first-party data with intent.

You must structure every page like it’s being read by an LLM.

Because it is.

The zero-click guest journey is rewriting your pipeline.

Three Bold Moves to Make Right Now

1️⃣ Build your website like it’s a database.

Schema-rich, structured, and written for AI engines.

Not humans alone.

2️⃣ Train every department in AI literacy.

This isn’t a marketing problem.

It’s an organizational fluency problem.

3️⃣ Treat your direct channel like an information system.

Not a booking engine.

A source of canonical truth for AI platforms.

The hotels that ignore this will keep wondering why conversions are down.

They’ll blame seasonality.

Meta shifts.

UX.

But the truth is simpler.

They’re no longer in the discovery layer.

They’ve disappeared.
