Sales Effectiveness at Scale with AI

Michael J. Goldrich · November 18, 2024

ZS Shows How AI Transforms Hotel Sales Training

At HSMAI’s Curate event in Washington, DC, ZS’s Sean Moran and Mike Francis delivered an eye-opening demonstration of how AI transforms sales training. Their presentation, “Sales Effectiveness at Scale,” showed a future where hotel sales teams can practice endlessly with AI-powered customers.

The Power of Practice
Moran opened with a sports analogy that resonated with the room. “Practice doesn’t make perfect. Perfect practice makes perfect,” he noted, channeling golf legend Gary Player’s famous quote, “The more I practice, the luckier I get.” Yet while athletes and performers practice constantly, sales teams rarely get the chance to rehearse crucial client interactions.

AI Changes the Game
The presentation included a live demo of an AI-powered training system. Mike Francis showed how sales professionals can practice client meetings with an AI “customer” named Jason Thompson, a corporate travel manager. The system evaluates everything from tone and professionalism to specific value propositions and problem-solving approaches.

What made this remarkable wasn’t just the technology – it was the practicality. Sales teams can now:

  • Practice difficult conversations without risk
  • Get immediate feedback on their performance
  • Learn new product features before real client meetings
  • Track improvement over time
  • Compare performance with peers

Beyond Basic Training
The system goes deeper than traditional role-playing. It understands industry context, remembers past interactions, and can simulate various customer personalities and scenarios. When Francis demonstrated a pricing discussion, the AI customer raised realistic objections and asked detailed follow-up questions.

Coaching at Scale Perhaps most impressive was the coaching component. The system helps sales managers:

  • Track team performance metrics
  • Identify areas needing improvement
  • Provide consistent feedback
  • Scale their coaching efforts across larger teams
  • Document progress and growth

The Human Element
Both speakers emphasized this technology aims to enhance rather than replace human sales professionals. “We’re freeing up time for what truly matters – building relationships and solving complex customer challenges,” Moran explained. The goal is handling routine training and practice so managers can focus on higher-value coaching.

The Future of Sales Training
The presentation sparked discussion about wider implications. One attendee asked about the “uncanny valley” effect of AI interactions. Moran acknowledged the technology isn’t perfect but emphasized its practical value today: “We’re already seeing dramatic improvements in sales performance, even as the technology continues to evolve.”

Real Results
ZS shared data showing sales teams using this approach:

  • Close more deals
  • Ramp up new hires faster
  • Handle objections more effectively
  • Show greater confidence in client meetings
  • Deliver more consistent messaging

For hotel sales leaders, the message was clear: AI-powered practice tools aren’t just coming – they’re here, and they’re transforming how teams learn, improve, and perform.
