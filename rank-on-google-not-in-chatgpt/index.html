Why does my business rank on Google and not appear in ChatGPT?

Michael J. Goldrich · September 2026

Because ranking and citation have come apart. Across 17 million AI citations Ahrefs analyzed, about 12 percent of the cited URLs ranked in Google's top ten for the question that produced them. Kirsten and colleagues studied 4,706 queries and found 53 percent of the domains Google's own AI Overviews cited were outside the organic top ten, and 27 percent were outside the top 100. Your rankings put your pages in the pool an assistant retrieves from. Whether a fragment of your page gets lifted into the answer depends on what the page says and how it is built. I see this pattern in most hotel audits at Vivander Advisors: strong SEO, a page-one position, and an empty seat on the shortlist.

Your analytics dashboard is not lying. It is answering a question nobody asks any more.

The two-year report that measured the wrong thing

A management company's agency had reported first-page rankings on every target keyword for two years. Accurate. Diligent. Green.

I ran fifty-four prompts for the flagship property. Recommended in two.

Nobody had done anything wrong by the rules of 2019. The rules moved, and the report kept reporting.

Is SEO still necessary?

Yes, and I want to be precise about why, because "SEO is dead" is as lazy as "SEO is enough."

Search rank is the second-best-evidenced factor in AI visibility, graded 9.4 on a 30-source analysis, behind only URL accessibility at 9.5. ChatGPT with search, Perplexity and Google's AI surfaces retrieve from search indexes. A page that cannot rank is rarely retrieved and therefore rarely cited.

SEO earns the crawl. It no longer earns the quote.

Why a ranking page is not a cited page

Search engines rank pages. Assistants select fragments.

A page built to rank for "luxury resort Clearwater" is organized around a keyword. Hero image. Brand paragraph. Amenities grid. Booking widget. It did its job for a decade.

A page built to be cited for "is there a beachfront resort in Clearwater with a kids' club and suites under $600" is organized around that question. There is a heading that asks it, and a first sentence that answers it. The rate in text, the kids' club by name.

Google's own grounding data shows the assistant lifting opening paragraphs almost whole and plateauing near 540 words. DEJAN's read of 883,262 snippets in 2026 found content survival falling from 61 percent for pages under a thousand words to 13 percent for pages over three thousand. The long, comprehensive page that SEO rewarded for ten years is the page AI reads an eighth of.

Why old pages get cited and your new ones do not

AI-cited pages average about 1,064 days old, per Ahrefs. Google's AI Overviews cite content slightly older than organic results do. Freshness lifted you in search. Citation does not care the same way.

So a 2019 article about your property sits comfortably in the middle of the AI citation distribution while your 2026 press release ranks and goes unread.

Why the platforms cite pages Google would never rank

ChatGPT's most-cited domain is Wikipedia, and it cites Google Maps heavily for best-of questions. Gemini and Perplexity lean on Reddit. For hospitality, OTAs frequently outrank all of them as cited sources, per Peec AI's 2026 data.

None of those appear in your rank tracker. Your rank tracker measures your pages. The assistant is reading everyone else's.

Three things worth sitting with

For your business. Keep the technical SEO. It is the retrieval layer. Then take the ten pages that matter and rebuild them around questions, answer first.

For your role. If you are being measured on rankings, you are being measured on the pool, not the answer. Ask for citation and rank side by side before your next review.

For your customers. They stopped scanning ten links. Now they read one answer. Page one of Google is a place they used to go.

What I do not know

Whether the decoupling widens as Google's AI Mode absorbs more of search, or narrows as the index and the answer converge. Overlap between AI Overview citations and organic results was 18 percent across two months in the Kirsten study. That figure will move, and I will say so when it does.

Where this is going

I told hotels for years that the fight was for the first page. And I was right for the era I was in. The era ended while the reports kept coming.

Google's AI queries already run about three times longer than traditional ones. A traveler enters one conversation and narrows through follow-up questions instead of running twelve searches. I call it the Answer Spiral. You appear early in that conversation or you never appear.

Page one still exists. It is a room the guest walked past on the way to the answer.
