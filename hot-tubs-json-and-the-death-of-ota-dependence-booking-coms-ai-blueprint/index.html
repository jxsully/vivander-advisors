Hot Tubs, JSON, and the Death of OTA Dependence: Booking.com’s AI Blueprint

Michael J. Goldrich · December 4, 2025

While most hotel teams are still debating whether AI belongs in guest experience, Booking.com is running billions of predictions a day, orchestrating agentic systems that surface personalized results, generate recommendations with purpose, and detect service issues before the guest ever calls.

They built a system that understands intent in natural language and responds with actions, not answers.

They track preference.

Parse context.

Route queries with 2x the precision.

➡️➡️ And yes … hot tubs were the #1 requested feature they weren’t even tracking until free-text filters revealed hidden demand.

Now?

Filter added.

Revenue unlocked.

That’s what AI literacy looks like.

This is about replacing guesswork with orchestration.

This is about websites functioning like databases, not brochures.

This is about building intelligent schema that powers both real-time agents and future-facing discovery systems.

Booking.com’s AI stack is more than impressive.

It’s strategic insulation from the OTA race to the bottom.

So what should hotel commercial teams actually do with this?

Stop obsessing over the next post or the last-minute campaign.

That’s not the battlefield anymore.

The new competition is happening inside LLMs, AI agents, and API calls.

Here’s your pivot:

Every direct channel is now competing with a generative interface.

That means your website isn’t just content.

It’s data.

Your team isn’t just marketing.

It’s training AI.

5 TAKEAWAYS FOR HOTEL COMMERCIAL TEAMS

1️⃣ Treat Your Website Like a Database

Content must be structured.

Comprehensive.

Semantic.

Searchable.

Think beyond pages.

Think schema.

AI doesn’t browse.

It parses.

2️⃣ Build AI Literacy Across the Team

AI mindset is no longer optional.

Upskill teams on prompt engineering, model selection, evaluation techniques.

This is sales and marketing in 2026.

3️⃣ Design for Agentic Interactions

Your direct channel must evolve from static to dynamic.

That means building workflows where queries lead to action, not dead ends.

4️⃣ Prioritize Use Cases that Unlock Revenue

Don’t chase flashy ideas.

Start with the boring problems that block conversion.

Reservation edits.

Upgrade paths.

Missed FAQs.

Automate them.

5️⃣ Think in JSON, Not Jargon

That’s not a metaphor.

If you want to play in AI search, your systems must output structured data.

That means investing in schema, tagging, and interoperable formats now.

Booking.com didn’t wait for AI to arrive.

They built for a world where memory matters, latency kills, and assistants (not users) decide who gets booked.

If your commercial team isn’t adapting to that yet, you’re already behind.
