The Surveillance Era of Search Is Here. Are You Ready to Compete?

Michael J. Goldrich · December 3, 2025

Google’s next move is knowing you through your Gmail, your Calendar, your Docs, your clicks, your silence.

The company’s new AI model, Gemini, is tapping into a deeply personalized web of user data.

It learns your preferences, anticipates your needs, and recommends products or decisions not just based on relevance but based on you.

Google’s VP of Search says this is the future: AI that doesn’t just answer questions but guides your decisions.

Seamlessly.

Invisibly.

Through every connected app you’ve forgotten is still running in the background.

Opt-out?

Technically possible.

Realistically? No.

The friction is too high.

And for many users, the convenience will outweigh the creepiness.

Google’s AI doesn’t show you the best product.

It shows you the one you’re most likely to buy.

NOW PIVOT TO HOTELS: WHAT THIS MEANS FOR COMMERCIAL TEAMS

If Google is using your guests’ private emails to decide what they’ll see next… then what chance does your hotel website have if it’s still built like a brochure?

The direct channel isn’t dying.

It’s being out-personalized.

You aren’t competing against OTAs.

You’re competing against Google’s hivemind.

And the truth is, most hotel websites are invisible to AI.

Not because they lack content, but because they lack structure.

If your website doesn’t have a comprehensive, layered schema it’s not a website, it’s a ghost.

The AI can’t read what it can’t parse.

And if your team isn’t AI-literate?

You’re not just behind.

You’re unarmed.

5 WAKE-UP ACTIONS FOR HOTEL COMMERCIAL TEAMS:

1️⃣ Train Your Teams in AI Literacy … Now

AI won’t wait for your next budget cycle.

If your revenue, marketing, or ecomm team doesn’t know how AI systems make decisions, you’re building campaigns blind.

2️⃣ Rebuild Your Website Like a Database

Think of your site not as pages, but as structured, queryable content.

Rich schema.

Embedded metadata.

Every page must be optimized for AI discovery, not just human eyes.

3️⃣ Create First-Party Data Strategies With Teeth

Google already has the guest’s intent.

If you don’t have first-party data that gives you behavioral context, your CRM is just a Rolodex.

4️⃣ Infuse AI Agents Into Your Booking Path

Static forms and FAQs won’t survive.

Voice agents.

Real-time AI guides.

Personalized nudges.

The experience needs to feel as smart as Google’s inbox.

5️⃣ Shift from Campaign Thinking to Signal Thinking

AI learns from behavior.

Your campaigns need to move in sync with guest signals not seasonality.

That means dynamic offers, responsive journeys, and content that evolves.

You’re not being replaced.

But you are being outrun.

The future of the direct channel isn’t about more ads or prettier photos.

It’s about becoming legible to AI.

Useful to humans.

And faster than both.
