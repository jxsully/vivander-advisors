What is RevGenAI Ops?

Michael J. Goldrich · September 2026

RevGenAI Ops is a hotel commercial role I proposed in a 2024 Hospitality Upgrade feature, The Transformative Role of RevGenAI Ops in Hospitality. One named owner for how generative AI runs across revenue management, marketing and distribution: which tools are in use, what they are allowed to decide, how their output is checked, and what result counts as proof. It answers the failure I have watched most often in hotel companies. Every stalled AI initiative shared the same root cause: nobody was named to own whether it worked, so a good result and a bad one got the same shrug.

The org chart is a lie, and AI already knows it. Organizations are preparing their people for a world that no longer exists.

Why a role rather than a committee?

Because committees own nothing.

AI tools now touch pricing, forecasting, guest communication, content, distribution and the website at once. Each department adopts them separately, on different tools, with different rules and no shared record. IT builds. HR trains. Nobody bridges the gap, and the pilot limps along forever.

A RevGenAI Ops owner keeps the inventory, writes the usage policy the teams helped draft, sets the proof criteria for each pilot, and reports the scorecard. It is as much governance as operations. I have started calling the function a workflow governor, because that is what it does: it decides where judgment stays human and where the agent runs.

What does the role own?

The AI tool inventory across the commercial function. Its usage policy and the decisions AI may and may not make. The proof criteria and the 30-day and 90-day scorecards for each pilot. Plus the AI visibility measurement: whether the property is recommended, per platform, and the fix list that follows. The agent-readiness of the booking path. And the literacy program that keeps the team current.

In a small property it is a hat one person wears. At a brand or management company it is a job with a title.

How does it relate to the Visibility Economy?

Directly. The Visibility Economy says discovery now happens in AI answers and the answer is built from sources most businesses never check. Someone has to check them, publish the facts, answer the questions and re-test. RevGenAI Ops is the seat that work sits in. Without it, an audit's fix list has no owner and decays in a quarter.

How does Vivander help set it up?

The AI Operating Review, a six-week advisory engagement, inventories what is already in use, writes the policy with the teams, sets the twelve-month plan and names the three roles that change. RevGenAI Ops is usually first. AI literacy programs train the person who takes the seat. The audit gives them their first work list.

Where this is going

Your AI colleague already joined the company. McKinsey reports 76 percent of executives now see agentic AI as a colleague. That mindset shift already separates the companies pulling ahead from the ones still focused on tools.

A colleague needs a manager. RevGenAI Ops is the manager. Somebody has to sign off on what the agent did last night.
