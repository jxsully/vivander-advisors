Why Don't Be Invisible

Customers stopped Googling. They started asking.

Whole categories are losing share of recommendation without realizing it, because the thing that replaced Google answers in complete sentences, not ten blue links. If you're not in the answer, you're not in the consideration set. The platform is the instrument you use to find out, and the engine you use to fix it.

How it works

Three loops. Running continuously.

Loop 01

Visibility tracking

Daily

We prompt the major answer engines with the questions your customers actually ask. Share of recommendation, positioning, and sentiment — tracked daily across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude.

Loop 02

Competitor monitoring

Real-time

We track the pages, schema, positioning, and content strategy your competitors are changing on their own sites. You see what they think is working — the moment they place the bet.

Loop 03

Action engine

Weekly

Gaps don't become insights until someone fixes them. Don't Be Invisible turns every detected gap into a specific, prioritized task — with the exact copy, schema, or content change we'd make.

Loop 04

Executive brief

Monthly

A short, written brief your executive team will actually read. What moved, why, what we'd do about it, what to escalate. Human-written, signed, and accountable.

What you get

Answers, not dashboards.

We are tired of tools that emit graphs and leave you holding the bag. The platform is built around the decisions an executive actually has to make.

01Live view

The Share-of-Recommendation board.

A single-screen view of how AI ranks you against your category — updated daily, segmented by the real questions your customers are asking.

02Diff feed

Competitor change log.

Every meaningful edit your competitors make to their public surface area — new pages, schema changes, repositioning, pricing. Annotated in plain English.

03Task engine

The gap-to-action queue.

A weekly, prioritized list of specific changes to make. Not "consider adding FAQs." More like: "Add this exact FAQ here, with this schema, because this is how the engines answer this question for Competitor B."

04Brief

A written executive brief.

Monthly, signed, human-authored. Your board can read it. Your team can act on it. No dashboards required.
