Hotels Built for Browsers Won’t Survive the Booking Bots.

Michael J. Goldrich · November 29, 2025

Bots from Google, Priceline, and Kayak are being trained to plan, monitor, and book travel.

Not suggest.

Not recommend.

Book.

80% of travel leaders plan to roll out agentic AI tools at scale within five years (McKinsey & Skift).

These bots will bypass your booking engine entirely.

If they can’t see your hotel

If they can’t parse your offer

If they can’t complete the reservation

You’re filtered out.

And you won’t even know it happened.

What hotel teams need to understand now:

Agentic AI doesn’t browse.

It selects.

And what it selects is based on structure, not style.

The direct channel is no longer a design problem.

It’s a data problem.

It’s an AI fluency problem.

And right now, the OTAs are more fluent than you.

5 moves commercial teams should make today:

1. Make your data machine-readable APIs, not PDFs.

Structured inventory.

Live pricing.

2. Train your team on AI behavior

They’re not dealing with humans.

They’re dealing with algorithms.

3. Deploy AI-native tools in your own stack

Voice agents.

Dynamic pricing.

Auto-personalized offers.

4. Write for algorithms, not eyes

Precision.

Clarity.

Logic over fluff.

5. Audit where your hotel shows up in AI-first platforms

If bots are your new buyer, how visible are you?

This is no longer about UX.

Agentic AI is not a channel.

It’s your new distribution partner.

And it’s already choosing winners.
