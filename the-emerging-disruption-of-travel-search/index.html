The Emerging Disruption of Travel Search

Michael J. Goldrich · March 13, 2025

OpenAI’s “Operator” and Its Impact on Hotelier Strategies

The travel industry is standing on the edge of a profound disruption, and hoteliers need to pay attention. OpenAI’s latest technology, Operator, is not just an innovation. It’s an emerging force that could dramatically reshape the way travel search and marketing operates. Currently in its early research phase, Operator is already making waves, with major travel players like Tripadvisor, Priceline, and Booking.com quickly integrating it into their platforms. As these industry giants move aggressively to incorporate this technology, hoteliers can’t afford to wait. This emerging AI technology is expected to change the very dynamics of travel search, and as it evolves, it will become increasingly disruptive.

Why Hoteliers Should Put Operator on Their Strategic Roadmap

Operator is an AI tool that transforms the traditional search and booking process by enabling agent-to-agent interactions. This means that customers, instead of directly engaging with websites and search engines like Google, will rely on AI agents powered by Operator to guide their travel decisions. As Operator becomes more refined, it’s expected that it will have a massive impact on how consumers search for, discover, and book travel.

The implications for hoteliers are significant. As Operator gains traction and becomes a more mainstream tool for travel bookings, the traditional search-and-booking model is poised for disruption. Operator will likely reduce reliance on direct website traffic and search engines as more customers rely on AI-driven recommendations.

For hoteliers looking to grow their direct channels, this shift is not something to be taken lightly. As more industry players aggressively embrace Operator, it will soon become a critical factor in how customers discover hotels. If you’re not paying attention to this now and incorporating it into your strategic roadmap, you risk falling behind as the industry adapts.

Five Strategic Steps Hoteliers Must Take Now

To stay competitive, hoteliers must take proactive steps to prepare for the future of travel search. Here are five key strategies for integrating Operator and other AI-powered search tools into your hotel marketing plan:

  1. Prioritize AI-Optimized Content for Agent Interactions
    • As Operator and other AI agents continue to evolve, your website’s content will need to be optimized for AI consumption. These agents are not just looking for traditional marketing messages.
    • They need data-rich, structured content that can be easily processed and understood. This means updating your website to include precise, clear, and reliable information that AI tools can use to make recommendations.
    • If your website’s content isn’t optimized for these agent interactions, it could get overlooked by AI-driven search.
  2. Invest in Generative AI for Personalization
    • AI-powered engagement isn’t just about responding to search queries.
    • It’s about delivering personalized, dynamic experiences. Hoteliers should invest in generative AI tools that allow for real-time, personalized engagement with guests.
    • Whether through custom offers, personalized messaging, or AI-powered chatbots, these technologies can help build a deeper connection with potential guests and drive bookings directly on your site.
    • With Operator driving AI-based interactions, those who can personalize the experience effectively will stand out.
  3. Possible New Digital Channel Opportunity: Cost-per-Agentic-Search
    • As AI agents take over the search function, traditional performance marketing models like cost-per-click (CPC) may become obsolete.
    • Instead, hoteliers should start thinking about cost-per-agentic-search or how to optimize their content and presence for AI-driven search agents.
    • The key here is building a trusted, authoritative presence in the AI ecosystem, so your hotel is more likely to be recommended by these agents.
    • By investing in strategies that improve your brand’s digital footprint, hoteliers can increase their chances of being discovered by AI agents.
  4. Rearchitect Your Website for AI-Agent Consumption
    • As AI-driven tools like Operator begin influencing how travelers find hotels, websites will need to evolve from static marketing assets to dynamic, AI-friendly platforms.
    • This means rethinking your site’s structure, making it easier for AI agents to extract data and interpret your offerings.
    • Structured data, clear APIs, and easy-to-read content will all be essential for AI agents to provide accurate, useful recommendations.
    • Investing in these changes now will ensure your site remains relevant as AI agents become a central part of the decision-making process.
  5. Build a Strong Brand Presence for AI Trust
    • AI agents are only as good as the information they rely on, and if your hotel isn’t positioned as a trusted, authoritative source, it could be left out of the conversation.
    • Focus on building a strong, recognizable brand that AI agents will want to prioritize.
    • Strengthen your online presence through reputation management, robust content, and high-quality media.
    • The more trust your brand builds in the digital space, the more likely AI agents will recommend you to potential guests.

The Time to Act is Now

Operator is not a technology of the distant future. It’s emerging now, and its potential for disruption is undeniable.

With large travel companies quickly incorporating this AI tool into their platforms, the shift in travel search will accelerate. For hoteliers, this means one thing: If you don’t have Operator and similar AI technologies on your strategic roadmap, you risk being left behind. The faster you act, the better positioned you will be to leverage AI agents and secure your place in the future of travel marketing. This is a critical moment for hoteliers to adapt, invest in AI, and future-proof their digital marketing strategies before the next wave of disruption hits.
