What is Vivander Advisors?

Michael J. Goldrich · September 2026

Vivander Advisors is the New York advisory firm that helps businesses get recommended by AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI surfaces. I founded it in March 2023. The firm runs AI visibility audits for hotels, restaurants and hospitality companies, rewrites the pages AI reads, trains teams to use AI in their own roles, advises leadership on AI decisions, and produces primary research and white papers for the hospitality industry. Vivander is a diagnostic firm, not an agency. The audit is written to be handed to any team, and I take no vendor referral fees.

I built it around a single thesis. AI is rewriting how customers discover brands, evaluate options and decide where to spend. Companies that were easy to find a year ago are becoming invisible to the systems that now mediate attention.

What does Vivander do?

Five things, and the first is the foundation.

The Vivander AI Visibility Audit tests 50 or more customer prompts per buyer persona across six AI platforms, three runs each, and reports where a business is named, cited, recommended or absent, with the sources behind every answer. Two to three weeks. Deliverables and fees are agreed for each engagement before work begins.

Answer Engine Content rewrites the pages AI reads so that every customer question has a heading that asks it and an answer that comes first, with prices and facts in visible text.

AI Literacy Programs and Agent Builder Sessions train hospitality teams on the tools they already use, and teach them to build their own AI agent systems without code.

AI Advisory helps owners, brands and management companies decide which AI initiatives to fund and how to govern them.

Research and Writing produces primary studies, white papers, business cases and bylined articles. I authored the hospitality industry foundation's research series on generative AI in commercial talent management.

Once the foundation is set, I can help with anything AI related. The order matters.

Who does Vivander work with?

Independent hotels, resorts, brands and management companies. Restaurant groups and independents. Spas, attractions and hospitality technology companies. Industry associations and their foundations. Marketing agencies that deliver the Vivander method under their own brand. Recent work includes Boston restaurant audits, a Florida beachfront resort, a sustainability products company and a direct-booking technology company.

Hospitality has been my living laboratory. The work applies anywhere visibility, trust and judgment still matter.

How is Vivander different from an agency or a platform?

Vivander is the check you run before you hire an agency, or before you decide you do not need one. I do not sell content work as a condition of the audit.

It is not a monitoring platform either. Platforms count mentions. I read whether you were offered, which is a different fact, and the gap between those two numbers is usually the whole story.

And I run my own practice on AI agents I built, on Claude Code, Firecrawl and Playwright. The research runs overnight. My time goes into verification and judgment. That is why the price is fixed and the turnaround is weeks, and it is why I can say "literacy before automation" without irony.
