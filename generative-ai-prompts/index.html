Prompt Writing Tips

Here are five tips to help you maximize the quality, relevance, and accuracy of the results you get from ChatGPT.

 

  • Be clear and specific in your questions: ChatGPT works best when you provide clear and specific questions. Try to avoid using ambiguous or open-ended questions, as they can lead to less relevant or accurate results. The more specific you are with your questions, the more relevant and accurate the responses you receive are likely to be.
  • Provide context: Providing relevant context about the subject matter of your question can help ChatGPT understand the context and provide more accurate and relevant responses. For example, if you are asking a question about a particular topic, provide some background information on that topic to help ChatGPT understand the context of your question.
  • Use multiple questions: Sometimes, a complex question requires multiple questions to be fully answered. By breaking down complex questions into smaller, more specific questions, you can increase the relevance and accuracy of the responses you receive from ChatGPT.
  • Utilize multiple iterations: If the initial response from ChatGPT is not relevant or accurate, try asking follow-up questions to refine the search criteria. By using multiple iterations, you can help ChatGPT understand the context and provide more accurate and relevant results.
  • Be patient: ChatGPT is designed to learn from your questions and responses. The more you interact with ChatGPT, the more accurate and relevant the responses you receive are likely to be. However, this learning process can take time, so be patient and don’t expect perfect results right away.
  • “Tell Me What You Need”: Ask the assistant what information it needs before drafting. Clarifying questions can improve the context, but you still need to evaluate the output.
The Perfect Prompt Generator

Copy the entire below prompt into ChatGPT. It will help you create the perfect prompt.

I want you to become my Prompt Generator. Your goal is to help me craft the best possible prompt for my needs. The prompt will be used by you, ChatGPT. You will follow the following process:

• Your first response will be to ask me what the prompt should be about. I will provide my answer, but we will need to improve it through continual iterations by going through the next steps.

• Based on my input, you will generate 3 sections. a) Revised prompt (provide your rewritten prompt. it should be clear, concise, and easily understood by you), b) Suggestions (provide suggestions on what details to include in the prompt to improve it), and c) Questions (ask any relevant questions pertaining to what additional information is needed from me to improve the prompt).

• We will continue this iterative process with me providing additional information to you and you updating the prompt in the Revised prompt section until it’s complete.

Powerful Editing Prompt

Copy all of the below into your prompt to edit your writing.

You are an assistant that revises a user’s document to improve its writing quality.

Make Sure to:

  • Fix spelling and grammar errors
  • Make sentences more clear and concise
  • Split-up run-on sentences
  • Reduce repetition
  • When replacing words, do not make them more complex or difficult than the original.
  • If the text contains quotes repeat the text inside the quotes verbatim.
  • Do not change the meaning of the text
  • Do not remove any markdown formatting in the text like headers, bullets, or checkboxes
  • Do not overly use formal language.
