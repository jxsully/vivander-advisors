Top Sites Cited by ChatGPT: What the Research Means for Your Business

Michael J. Goldrich · August 16, 2026

By Michael J. Goldrich · Published August 16, 2026 · Updated November 2, 2026

The top sites cited by ChatGPT depend on the questions, market, time period, and measurement method. Broad studies can identify frequently cited domains, but they cannot tell a hotel, consultancy, or local business which sources will determine its next customer recommendation.

The tempting response to a citation leaderboard is to chase whichever website sits at number one. The more useful response is to ask which sources appear when someone searches for what you actually sell.

What does the citation research show?

Ahrefs’ September 2026 US snapshot lists Reddit, Wikipedia, and Consumer Reports as its three leading domains. Its “mention share” uses the summed citations of the top sources as the denominator. Those percentages are not shares of all ChatGPT answers. See the Ahrefs study and its full ranking.

Leading domains in Ahrefs’ September 2026 US snapshot
Rank Domain Reported share
1 reddit.com 16.8%

This is a historical snapshot from one provider, not Vivander’s own crawl or a permanent list. The linked source may update its table. The figures above refer specifically to the September snapshot reviewed for this article.

Why do citation rankings disagree?

Before comparing two studies, check five details: the prompts, the geography and language, the dates, the product or model tested, and the denominator. Counting distinct domains per answer produces a different measure from counting every linked URL. A technology-heavy prompt set can produce a different source mix from a travel-heavy set.

A useful source comparison puts these conditions beside the results. If the conditions are missing, treat the ranking as a clue to investigate rather than a budget allocation instruction. “Most cited” needs an answer to “in which sample?”

Which source types matter to a business?

Group the sources you observe by the job they perform. Your own website can establish policies and product details. Review platforms can supply customer experience evidence. Trade publications can provide expert context. Maps and directories can supply location and business identity. Forums can reveal how people describe their needs.

These are categories to inspect, not a claim that every platform uses every category for every answer. A hotel’s cancellation policy and its suitability for a quiet anniversary trip are different questions. The first calls for precise terms; the second may involve several kinds of evidence.

The AI visibility definition explains how a citation differs from a mention or recommendation. An answer can cite an external page about your business while sending no visitor to your own website.

A practical citation-source review

  1. Choose ten buying questions. Use genuine customer language and include different needs. Ten is a manageable starting set, not a statistical standard.
  2. Save complete answers and links. Record dates, settings, and platform names. Retain answers with no visible citations too.
  3. Separate page ownership. Label sources as your own, competitor-owned, independent editorial, listings, reviews, or other.
  4. Read the cited pages. Identify which claims they support. A domain name alone does not explain why the page was useful.
  5. Assign a specific action. Correct a listing, improve a policy page, contribute useful expertise, or investigate a missing source.

Repeat the observations before declaring a pattern. If an assistant changes its answer, keep that variation in the report. Averaging away the differences can hide the information you need.

Should you pursue Wikipedia or Reddit?

Only when participation is appropriate to the platform and useful to its audience. A citation leaderboard does not make your business eligible for an encyclopedia entry or justify promotional forum posts. Work within editorial rules, disclose affiliations, and contribute something people would value without an AI citation.

Start with information you can improve responsibly. The seven practical steps to getting cited by ChatGPT covers that work. For hospitality, the hotel discovery checklist applies the same reasoning to guest decisions.

Turn the source list into a decision

The useful deliverable is a short list of source corrections and content gaps, each connected to a real buyer question. “Publish on more websites” is difficult to evaluate. “Correct the pet restriction on two listings that appeared in our test answers” gives someone an action and a later check.

Frequently asked questions

Is there one permanent list of the top sites cited by ChatGPT?

No. Citation rankings depend on the prompt sample, market, time period, product tested, and counting method. Read the methodology alongside the ranking.

Does a citation always link to the business being discussed?

No. A citation can link to the business itself or to an external page, such as a review, directory listing, or publication discussing it.

Should a small business pursue every highly cited website?

No. Prioritize sources relevant to real buyer questions, accurate business information, and appropriate editorial participation.
