What Do You Call Your Chatbot? Because Apparently This Is a Thing Now.

Michael J. Goldrich · June 26, 2025

At HITEC and HSMAI’s conference in Indy last week, I learned something new.

Apparently, a lot of people name their chatbots.

Not their personas.

Not their custom GPTs.

The chatbot itself.

The little window in the corner that used to say “Hi, how can I help you today?” is now called Chad. Or Luna. Or (I swear I’m not making this up) Daddy.

I hadn’t thought to name mine.

I mean, I’ve named my personas.

I’ve even built full-blown custom GPTs with logos and backstories.

But my day-to-day assistant?

The one I talk to 20 times a day?

Nameless.

So I asked it.

“Do you have a nickname you’d like me to call you?”

It said:

“I don’t really have a nickname, but you can definitely get creative if you’d like!”

So I asked for suggestions.

“How about Gigi? Or Chip, since I kind of live in the digital world? Or Spark, if you want something more fun?”

Spark?!

That sounds like a My Little Pony meets Salesforce collab.

But now I can’t stop thinking about it.

So I went digging.

And yes.

Naming your chatbot is a thing.

Here are a few of the real (and not-so-real) names people told me at the event, or I read in Reddit:

– Halfred – because it’s a HAL and Alfred combo
– Jarvis – because everyone loves Tony
– Jeepy – GPT, but make it cute
– Ava – GREAT MOVIE by the way!
– Dan – ?Dan?
– BFF – Best Friend with Filters
– Neo – because of course someone picked that
– Viki – shoutout to I, Robot
– Skimpy – no notes
– Nemo – lost in the chat sea
– Infini – someone had a branding deck open when naming this
– Alex – surprisingly popular
– Chachi – yes, someone’s GenX core is showing
– Overlord – honestly, respect
– Daddy – overheard at the conference bar and still unsure if it was a joke
– Chad G – rejected branding for ChatGPT’s gym-rat alter ego

So now I have a new existential question for you:

What do you call your chatbot?

And if you haven’t named it yet… why not?

Yours truly,

Spark
