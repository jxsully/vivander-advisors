What is the Visibility Economy?

Michael J. Goldrich · September 2026

The Visibility Economy is the framework I developed at Vivander Advisors to describe how businesses get found when AI answers the question instead of a search engine listing links. Three forces drive it. Organic search click-through is declining as AI Overviews, answer boxes and ads absorb attention. Answer engines are rising as people ask whole questions and receive one answer instead of ten links. And AI channels are forming as research, reservations and bookings begin to route through assistants and agents. In that economy, visibility no longer means ranking first. It means being cited, recommended or surfaced in answers people treat as authoritative. The framework underlies Invisible: What To Do When AI Erases Your Business and every Vivander audit.

Visibility equals power. That sentence used to be a slogan. It is becoming a balance sheet.

What are the three forces?

SEO decay. Organic click-through falls as the results page fills with AI summaries, sponsored placements and answer features. When an AI Overview appears, organic clicks drop sharply. The page that ranks first receives a smaller share of the attention it used to command.

Answer engine ascent. Users type questions, not keywords, and read an answer, not a list. Google's AI search queries run about three times longer than traditional ones. A traveler enters one conversation and narrows through follow-ups. I call it the Answer Spiral. One session, zero clicks, and the property either appears early in that spiral or never appears.

AI channel emergence. Assistants and agents are beginning to complete the task. Plan the trip, compare the rates, make the booking. Brands absent from AI answers lose consideration before a customer reaches any website, and there is no bid to raise.

What is the Visibility Stack?

The four layers a business has to pass, in my model, to appear in AI answers. SEO: can the pages be found and ranked, which is the pool assistants retrieve from. AEO: do the pages answer questions in a form a machine can lift. GEO: how AI models represent the brand, mostly through third-party sources. AI visibility: what comes out when a customer asks, measured per platform.

A gate sits at the entrance: retrieval. Any business absent from citations entirely is failing retrieval, and the fix is access and indexation, not content. Most of the market is selling content optimization on the wrong side of that gate.

What is the visibility ladder?

Five bands from the book, used across every Vivander score. Invisible. Reactive. Structured. Integrated. Optimized. Twenty-point intervals on a 0 to 100 scale. Most businesses I audit for the first time land in the first two bands, and most of them had been told they were doing fine.

How is the Visibility Economy different from SEO?

SEO optimized a page to rank for a keyword and collect a click. The Visibility Economy asks whether a fragment of the page will be lifted into an answer and whether the business will be offered. Rank and citation have separated: only about 12 percent of AI-cited URLs sit in Google's top ten for the question. SEO remains the retrieval layer. It stopped being the whole job.

What should a business do about it?

Measure per platform, with repeated runs. Confirm retrieval before rewriting anything. Put the answer first under a heading that asks the question, with the price in text. Get corroborated on the sources each platform reads for the category. Make the booking path readable by an agent. For audit clients who want follow-up retests, repeat the questions at 60 and 90 days. These retests are optional, not automatic.

Literacy before automation. Visibility before transaction. Understanding before tactics. That order is the framework in nine words.
