The Power of In-Person Meetings

Michael J. Goldrich · November 21, 2024

Unlocking 36% Revenue Growth – Backed by Accor Research

In a business world increasingly dominated by digital interactions, the timeless value of face-to-face meetings remains unshaken. While virtual platforms have bridged geographic gaps, Accor’s latest global research reveals that in-person meetings offer unparalleled advantages, with the potential to increase revenue by an astounding 36%.

This insight underlines a compelling truth: while digital tools are effective, they cannot fully replicate the trust, collaboration, and connection built through human interaction.

The Research: Why In-Person Meetings Matter

Accor’s study, surveying over 9,000 professionals globally, provides robust evidence that face-to-face engagements are critical drivers of business success. Respondents expressed a belief that their revenue potential could soar if pivotal meetings, negotiations, client onboarding, and strategic collaborations, were conducted in person.

  • Impact Comparison: Professionals equate the value of one in-person meeting to three virtual meetings, highlighting the greater efficacy of direct interactions.
  • Commercial Drivers: The primary motivation for business travel remains commercial, sealing deals, strengthening partnerships, and engaging stakeholders.

Sophie Hulgard, Chief Sales Officer at Accor, succinctly captures this sentiment:

“Digital is powerful, but face-to-face is valuable.”

Beyond Numbers: The Human Connection Advantage

In-person meetings foster trust, a cornerstone of successful relationships. The nuances of body language, tone, and shared experiences create a depth of connection that virtual platforms struggle to match. Here’s how face-to-face interactions excel:

  • Building Trust: Trust is more easily established when parties interact in person, making negotiations smoother and outcomes more favorable.
  • Strengthening Collaboration: Team dynamics flourish in physical meetings, where brainstorming and problem-solving are more organic.
  • Creating Memorable Experiences: Personal engagement leaves a lasting impression, enhancing client relationships and boosting loyalty.

Business Implications: A Billion-Dollar Opportunity

Accor’s findings reveal that embracing in-person meetings could unlock billions in global economic growth. For organizations, this isn’t merely a tactical shift but a strategic imperative. The 36% potential revenue boost sends a clear message: businesses that prioritize human connection will outperform their competition.

Adapting to a New Era: The Phygital Strategy

In today’s evolving marketplace, a balanced “phygital” approach, combining the efficiency of digital with the impact of physical meetings, is the way forward. Businesses can maximize their outcomes by adopting the following strategies:

  1. Strategically Allocate Resources: Reserve in-person meetings for high-value interactions, such as client onboarding, negotiations, and strategic planning.
  2. Enhance Meeting Quality: Invest in venues and environments that foster creativity and collaboration, ensuring every meeting achieves its purpose.
  3. Leverage Technology for Hybrid Efficiency: Use virtual tools for preliminary discussions, saving in-person engagements for moments requiring maximum impact.

Prioritize People, Maximize Potential

As Accor’s research shows, business is ultimately about people. Companies that recognize the transformative power of face-to-face meetings are positioning themselves to thrive in an era of rapid change. By finding the right balance between virtual efficiency and in-person engagement, businesses can drive trust, close high-value deals, and achieve sustainable growth.
