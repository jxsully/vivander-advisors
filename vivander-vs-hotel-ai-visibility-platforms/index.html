Vivander vs hotel AI visibility platforms: what is the difference?

Michael J. Goldrich · September 2026

A new category of software has formed around hotels and AI answers. In June 2026 I asked ChatGPT for "AI discoverability consultants for hotels." The answer named eight platforms, among them Rebean, Tharro, Bezeen, DominateAI, Visaible, Hotelrank, Palmtree.ai and a hospitality content-distribution platform, each describing itself as monitoring, optimizing or executing a hotel's AI visibility. It did not name me. That result is on this page because I ran the audit I sell on my own domain and published what it found. Vivander Advisors is not a platform. It is the independent diagnostic a hotel runs before it chooses one, or before it decides it does not need one: an audit with the retrieval gate, the recommendation-event standard, a published formula and no software to sell afterward. Several of those platforms are complementary to that work. One hospitality content-distribution platform is a research partner.

Zero of five prompts. My own firm, invisible in the category it wrote the book on. If I can say that out loud, I can be trusted to read your results honestly.

What the platforms offer, in their own words

Reading their public descriptions in mid-2026: monitoring how AI systems describe a property and returning insights to improve direct bookings; tracking visibility across Google, social, OTAs and AI assistants with weekly action plans; ensuring a hotel is recognized and recommended by ChatGPT; tracking how AI recommends the hotel and providing strategies to win prompts; auditing how AI travel planners see a business; and in the hospitality content-distribution platform’s case, distributed editorial mentions that drive AI recommendations and direct bookings.

I have not audited these products. What I describe comes from their own sites. Every hotel should test any claim, mine included, against its own prompt set.

Where Vivander differs

Independence. I sell no software, take no referral fees and do not require the content work as a condition of the audit. The fix list is written to be handed to any team or any platform.

The retrieval gate. A page nothing retrieves drives no content recommendation, because rewriting it can reduce visibility. I check crawler access, the CDN toggle and the initial HTML before anything else. A platform that reports "not recommended" and sells "optimization" without that check is selling the wrong fix first.

The recommendation-event standard. I count a hotel as visible only when it is offered to the asker. Appearance counts and recommendation counts are different numbers, and the gap between them is where most dashboards flatter.

The published method. Every score prints its formula, its sample, its stability rate and its limits. And every row carries the quoted passage from the live page.

Hospitality operating depth. I ran the direct channel at three hotel companies and served as Chief Experience Officer of a direct-booking technology company. That is why the booking handoff check exists.

Where a platform may be the right choice

For continuous monitoring after the diagnosis. Or for a portfolio that needs weekly tracking across dozens of properties. For editorial distribution at scale, where the hospitality content-distribution platform’s model of placed mentions addresses the off-site layer directly. The audit tells a hotel which of those it needs, and my recommendation is sometimes "not now; fix the site first."

How Vivander and a hospitality content-distribution platform work together

We run a joint working group on measuring AI recommendation: what counts, how to measure it, and the rubric that separates a brand being named from a brand being offered, presented at Destination AI in September 2026. The measurement standard is shared. Their services are different from mine.

Where this is going

The category will consolidate. Platforms will add diagnosis and diagnostics will add monitoring, and the measurement standard the industry settles on will decide who was counting the right thing all along.

I would rather be the firm that published its own zero than the one that hid it. That is the whole pitch, and it is also the method.
