AI Will Create Smarter Jobs. Don’t Get Left Behind.

Michael J. Goldrich · June 24, 2025

In The New York Times article, “22 New Jobs A.I. Can Give You,” Robert Capps outlines how AI is creating entirely new ones in trust, integration, and taste.

The core idea:
➡️ AI handles tasks.
➡️ Humans take responsibility.
➡️ And the future belongs to those who can direct, verify, and shape what AI produces.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR HOTEL COMMERCIAL TEAMS

For hotels, this isn’t a future conversation.

It’s now.

AI is already transforming how guests discover, evaluate, and book especially through voice, search, and content.

If your team isn’t actively learning how to guide and audit AI, you’re not just missing opportunities.

You’re falling behind.

The direct channel won’t survive on automation alone.

It will grow when your team knows how to lead AI with purpose.



FIVE ACTION STEPS TO STAY COMPETITIVE

1️⃣ Build AI Fluency Across the Team

Everyone touching content, pricing, or guest communication should know how to prompt, evaluate, and refine AI outputs.

2️⃣ Assign Ownership

Treat AI tools like any staff member. Someone needs to be responsible for what they say and do, especially in direct guest channels.

3️⃣ Integrate AI With Direct Booking Goals

Use AI to power campaign workflows, voice agents, and personalized offers that directly support your conversion funnel.

4️⃣ Create Reusable Systems

Build internal playbooks so campaigns, guest replies, and upsell flows can be deployed quickly and adjusted instantly.

5️⃣ Lead With Vision, Not Volume

AI can create. But it’s your team’s taste that gives it meaning. Focus on brand voice, guest intent, and emotional clarity.
