AI Is Rewriting the Booking Journey. You’re Not in the First Draft.

Michael J. Goldrich · December 1, 2025

Booking.com just gave you the playbook.

They’re proudly showing how generative AI is being built into every layer of
the guest journey:

➡️ AI trip planning
➡️ Smart property filters
➡️ Instant Q&A tools
➡️ Automated replies
➡️ AI-generated review summaries
➡️ AI-driven onboarding

Their own research says nearly 50% of travelers are already using GenAI to plan their trips.

AI is becoming the first point of influence.

Before search.

Before brand.

Before price.

When a traveler asks ChatGPT, or Google’s new AI assistant, or even Booking.com‘s trip planner:

“Where should I stay in Lisbon with my partner, walking distance to local bars, but not too loud?”

They’re not going to your website.

They’re going to whatever source trained the model.

And that source?

It’s your OTA listings.

The irony is brutal.

You’re painstakingly crafting room descriptions, uploading high-quality photos, responding to guest questions manually and every ounce of that effort is helping Booking.com train its AI to replace the need for you.

They’re turning content into insight.

Turning interactions into prediction.

And turning hotels into interchangeable, AI-sorted options that fit the query.

You’re not just competing with other hotels.

You’re competing with the interface that decides whether you even show up.

🧐 What Should You Do?

This is the moment hotel commercial teams need to stop thinking about content as marketing.

And start treating it like AI fuel.

Here are five things every hotel team should be doing now:

1️⃣ Build for discoverability inside LLMs, not just search engines.

These systems prioritize relevance, not keywords.

You need detailed, emotionally intelligent content that answers human queries.

2️⃣ Turn your direct site into an AI-native experience.

Build a vibe-based search that mirrors how travelers think.

3️⃣ Create with intent, not repetition.

Travelers are asking AI for: “best place to work remotely in Crete with good Wi-Fi and a pool view.” Does your content speak to that?

Or is it still listing “amenities include: pool, Wi-Fi, desk”?

4️⃣ Upskill your team. Not with AI tools. With AI thinking.

Anyone can learn ChatGPT.

Your team needs to learn to think in prompts, in scenarios, in possibilities.

The best results come from the best questions.

5️⃣ Reclaim your leverage. Stop feeding the machine for free.

Every photo, every review reply, every listing update you do on Booking.com trains their model.

Start creating parallel content on your direct site that is richer, more emotional, more specific.

❗ Final Word:

The OTAs are teaching guests to plan with AI.

They’re training algorithms to match travelers to properties.

They’re turning your operational excellence into their product advantage.
