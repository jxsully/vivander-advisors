Vivander vs an SEO or GEO agency vs doing it yourself: which should a business choose?

Michael J. Goldrich · September 2026

Run the diagnosis first, then decide. Vivander Advisors is the check a business runs before it hires an SEO or GEO agency, or before it decides it does not need one: a scoped audit that finds out whether AI recommends the business, why not, and what to fix in what order, written to be handed to any team. An agency sells the ongoing work and has a reason to find work to sell. A business with an editor and an afternoon can ship the first three fixes on most of my lists itself. The honest answer to "which one" is usually the audit, then your own team for a month, then a decision about whether you need an agency for the off-site layer.

Every leadership team needs an independent perspective on what their agencies are actually doing. That is not an insult to agencies. It is how you find out whether the retainer is working.

Why not go straight to an agency?

Because the most common expensive mistake in this field is content work on a page that was never retrieved. A 2026 study found body-only optimization reduced citation. An agency that opens with a content calendar has not checked the Cloudflare toggle, the robots file or the initial HTML. I have found the whole problem there more than once.

Agencies also tend to sell the things the evidence retired. Schema as a citation lever. FAQ markup. llms.txt. Self-published rankings. The audit's fix list says which of those to decline, with the study attached.

Why not do it all yourself?

You can do more of it than agencies suggest. Fifteen unbranded prompts, three runs on two platforms across two days, classified honestly, will tell you where you stand. Facts into text and questions into headings are editor work. I say so on every page of this site, because it is true and because it is the part of the method that costs nothing.

What is hard alone is the access check across a dozen crawlers, the heading-to-question map across a whole site, the source sweep per platform, and knowing which findings in this field are real. I built the tooling to do those overnight and I read the evidence for a living.

When an agency is the right answer

When the diagnosis says the gap is off-site and you have no editorial relationships. Or when there are dozens of properties and no internal capacity. When the site needs a rebuild for agent readiness. The audit names the gap, and a good agency takes the fix list and runs it. I deliver my method under partner agency brands for exactly that reason.

What I will not do

Sell the content work as a condition of the audit. Take a referral fee for recommending anyone. Recommend a rewrite before confirming retrieval. Report a number without its denominator.

Where this is going

The retainer model was built for a game with a scoreboard everyone could read. Today's scoreboard is six answer engines that disagree with each other, and the only honest way to read it is to rerun the prompts. Agencies that adopt that discipline will earn their retainers. Those that keep reporting rankings will be reporting the pool.

Start with the diagnosis. If the result is fine, you will know. And if it is not, you will know what to fix first.
