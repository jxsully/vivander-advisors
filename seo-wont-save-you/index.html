SEO Won’t Save You

Michael J. Goldrich · July 17, 2025

Why Hotels Are Invisible in AI Search and How to Fix It

Your old digital playbook, optimize, rank, earn clicks, kept rooms full for more than a decade. That playbook now misfires quietly while guests plan trips with ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google’s AI Overviews. Those systems do not rank; they answer. If your property is not named in that answer, you disappear.

The Shift From Search Engines to Answer Engines

Google’s AI Overviews appeared in just 6.49 percent of U.S. queries this January. By March the share more than doubled to 13.14 percent, with travel among the most affected categories.(Hospitality Technology) Every time an Overview fires, organic clicks drop.

Meanwhile 72 percent of consumers now use generative AI tools regularly, and nine percent already trust AI more than any human source when deciding what to buy.(Accenture) Travelers feed a prompt, see an answer, and move on. Zero blue links, zero suspense.

Traditional SEO still matters. It gets pages crawled. It does not get you quoted. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) does.

What AI Wants

AI models compress thousands of pages into the smallest high‑confidence statement possible. They choose:

  • Clear, structured sentences.
  • Facts repeated across trusted sites.
  • Content they can parse without gimmicks.

If five independent sources call your hotel the best honeymoon spot in Santorini, the model repeats it. If your site hides that claim behind brand flourish or accordion menus, it skips you.

How to Rebuild Visibility

1. Structure First

Ask the question in an H2 or H3, then answer it in the next two lines. Bold a one‑sentence summary. The model should never hunt.

2. Add Schema

Implement FAQ, HowTo, Article, and Hotel schema. Google’s AI Overviews lean heavily on structured data to verify context.

3. Simplify Copy

Write like you speak to an intelligent friend. Short sentences. Everyday words. No brand poetry. Bullets and numbered lists help models extract answers.

4. Earn Repetition

Get listed in top‑ten roundups, local guides, and destination blogs. AI trusts consensus, not corporate self‑praise. Shift PR from backlink hunting to mention hunting.

5. Fix Technical Roadblocks

Keep answers in raw HTML. Avoid text hidden by tabs, infinite scroll, or heavy JavaScript. If a crawler stalls, the quote never surfaces.

Measuring Success Without Clicks

SEO tracked rankings, sessions, and conversions. AEO tracks presence.

  • Answer Presence: Search your hotel in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Are you explicitly named?
  • Third‑Party Mentions: Monitor new features in trusted travel outlets, not just backlinks.
  • Schema Coverage: Audit every key page.
  • Quote Readiness: Evaluate whether each page provides a stand‑alone, two‑line answer.

Click‑through rate will fall as Overviews rise, but appearing in the answer keeps you in the guest’s head when they hand off to booking engines or brand sites.

Direct Channel Implications

Traffic used to be the metric. Presence now matters more. When the AI summary lists your property, brand awareness jumps without a single site visit. Booking then flows through direct, OTA, or voice assistant depending on the guest’s next action. Fight to own the mention and retarget through first‑party data you already control, email, app, loyalty.

Your Six‑Step AEO Action Plan

  1. Rewrite existing pages around clear Q & A.
  2. Roll out schema across the whole site, starting with high‑intent pages.
  3. Trim marketing fluff; surface facts fast.
  4. Secure external features that repeat your key claims.
  5. Track answer visibility monthly across leading AI tools.
  6. Maintain technical SEO while shifting budget toward AEO experiments.

Move Early, Win Early

AI travel planning is not tomorrow’s problem. Perplexity now pulls from a billion Tripadvisor reviews and produces full itineraries in seconds.(Tom’s Guide) Google’s Overviews widen every month. Travelers have already crossed the bridge from keyword search to answer search.

I help hotel teams cross the same bridge, upgrading solid SEO foundations with AEO tactics that put their properties inside the answer box. If your name does not show up when ChatGPT recommends where to stay, let’s change that.
