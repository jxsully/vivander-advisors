The New Guest: Why AI Will Decide Who Wins in Hospitality

Michael J. Goldrich · July 16, 2025

There’s a fundamental shift underway in how guests choose hotels, and most commercial teams are not prepared.

For years, hotel marketers and sales teams believed they understood the customer journey. Guests were human. Decisions were emotional. Success came from driving awareness through search, social media, advertising, and loyalty. That world is ending.

New research from Accenture reveals that your next guest might not be human at all. Generative AI is not just supporting travelers in decision-making. It is beginning to make their decisions for them.

According to Accenture’s 2025 Consumer Pulse Report:

  • 72 percent of consumers now use generative AI tools regularly in their lives
  • 36 percent of active users describe AI as a “good friend”
  • 87 percent say they would ask AI for advice on social and relationship matters
  • 9 percent already trust AI as their most reliable source for what to buy
  • AI now ranks as the second most influential source of purchase recommendations for active users, behind only physical stores
  • 75 percent of consumers are ready to let AI agents act as personal shoppers and make purchases on their behalf
  • 34 percent of consumers say they would switch from a preferred brand if another made them feel more special or valued

What this means is simple and alarming. AI agents are evolving from tools to consumers. They are intermediating decisions. They are executing purchases. And they are bypassing traditional marketing touchpoints in the process.

If your hotel is not discoverable by an AI agent, you will not be seen by the human guest it represents.

The traditional playbook is becoming obsolete. Optimizing for search engines and social feeds is no longer enough. You are no longer speaking only to human guests. You are speaking to the systems that serve them.

AI-driven agents now manage discovery, recommendation, selection, and purchase. They decide who enters the consideration set. They filter brand messages. They remove friction. They make booking decisions. And unless you act, your hotel could be excluded automatically.

Visibility now depends on whether your content, offers, and data can be parsed and prioritized by AI language models and recommendation engines.

What used to be Search Engine Optimization is giving way to Generative Engine Optimization. AI systems are trained on structured data, reliable signals, and authentic narratives. Hotels without clean, structured, and consistently updated content will simply be invisible.

At the same time, the concept of personalization is being redefined. AI agents are not influenced by digital ads, influencer marketing, or loyalty points. They act based on pattern recognition, guest history, and their owner’s preferences. They optimize for trust, efficiency, and outcomes.

To win, your hotel must be chosen by a machine acting on behalf of your future guest. This requires new thinking and immediate action.

First, your content must be machine-readable and emotionally resonant. Everything about your hotel from amenities to brand story must be presented in ways AI agents can parse and understand. Every offer must be distinct enough to stand out in machine logic while still evoking emotional value for the human traveler.

Second, your digital infrastructure must support real-time, accurate, and agent-friendly booking. Clean APIs, up-to-date rates, and transparent inventory exposure are not optional. AI agents will avoid brands with inconsistent data. They will default to hotels that deliver seamless integration.

Third, your marketing strategy must pivot from shouting louder to being discoverable inside AI ecosystems. This is not a future scenario. This is a commercial reality already unfolding.

Consider that half of active AI users have already made purchase decisions influenced by AI. That number will rise. According to Accenture, brands that fail to define their role within the LLM ecosystem risk being passively represented or left out entirely.

The AI agent does not care about your last digital ad campaign. It cares about data clarity, recommendation strength, and whether your offer meets its optimization criteria.

Your hotel’s next guest may never see your website. Their AI agent will.

Commercial teams must stop asking how to drive human traffic and start asking how to win machine preference.

This is not an incremental change. It is a wholesale shift in how bookings are made.

Hotels that act now will secure their place in AI-driven commerce. Those that wait will simply fall out of consideration, silently and automatically.

Ask yourself: If an AI agent were choosing your next guest’s hotel today, would it choose you?

If the answer is no, your revenue is at risk.

The future of hospitality belongs to those who understand that the guest is changing. You are no longer selling to humans alone.

You are selling to their AI.

And the time to pivot is now.
