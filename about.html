About

An advisory practice, led by an operator.

Vivander Advisors was founded on a stubborn belief: that good companies deserve advisors who've actually done the job — not people selling frameworks they've never had to run a P&L against.

The founder

Michael J. Goldrich.

Former Chief Experience Officer at The Hotels Network, with prior senior roles across a quarter-century of commercial leadership: Starwood Hotels & Resorts, Wyndham, Club Quarters, Accenture, and Monster.

Author of three books on AI and modern leadership: Invisible: What to Do When AI Erases Your Business, The AI Literacy Playbook, and Too Many Hats, Too Little Time.

Named to HSMAI's list of the Top 25 Most Extraordinary Minds in Hospitality. Multiple Adrian Award winner. Regular columnist for Hospitality Upgrade. Frequent speaker at HSMAI, HITEC, Skift, NYU, and Cornell.

Based in New York. Engagements worldwide.

The firm

Why Vivander Advisors exists.

After two decades inside operating businesses — running commercial teams, P&Ls, and digital transformations — I kept watching the same thing happen with AI. Companies would spend serious money on strategies that never met a customer, never moved a number, and never survived the next reorganization. Good companies. Smart executives. Wasted quarters.

The pattern wasn't technical. It was operational. The senior team couldn't have a substantive conversation about what the technology actually changed. The pilots couldn't survive without someone protecting them. The category was shifting underneath the business, and nobody could see it clearly because the map was being drawn by vendors.

Vivander exists because that's fixable — if you have someone in the room who has actually done the job, says what they think, and knows how to turn a thesis into a functioning workflow.

How we work.

Small, senior, and stubbornly focused. Every engagement is run by a partner with operating experience — not a rotating cast of juniors. We stay small on purpose. It's the only way we know how to do work we're willing to put our name on.

We don't staff against headcount. We scope against outcomes. And we tell clients, clearly, when we think they don't need us — which happens more often than you'd expect, and is the main reason the ones who do come back.

Where we're deepest.

Hospitality and travel are our home category — hotels, resorts, multi-property groups, and the commercial operators around them. We work across adjacent service-intensive industries too: professional services, specialty retail, and multi-site operating businesses where AI has to meet a physical, human, messy reality.
