OTAs Will Own the AI Results. You Will Pay to Compete.

Michael J. Goldrich · November 27, 2025

Google is now inserting sponsored ads inside AI Search Mode.

These are embedded directly into the AI-generated answers.

One line below the response, labeled “sponsored,” positioned for now at the bottom.

The test is already live.

HVAC companies are showing up.

Local services are the first to be pushed into this monetized layer.

As soon as Google completes its data review on performance and engagement, expect hospitality to follow.

What This Means for Hotels

AI will become the gatekeeper to guest intent.

A guest searches for “boutique hotel near the beach,” and AI generates the answer.

If a paid ad takes the final spot, your hotel is no longer visible.

If OTAs win that slot, the direct booking dies on the spot.

This is a shift in power.

From hotels to AI platforms.

From brands to brokers.

Visibility will belong to the bidder.

Not the best product.

Not the guest-preferred experience.

Just whoever pays to appear.

Your booking path now depends on how well your team understands how AI curates, ranks, and delivers information.

This is a closed content system.

Monetized.

Controlled.

Already tilting toward big ad buyers.

What Smart Commercial Teams Do Next

This is about distribution architecture.

Your direct revenue is at the mercy of the AI layer.

You have control over exposure inside generative summaries.

That is where your playbook needs to evolve.

5 Strategic Shifts to Make Now

1️⃣ Train Your Commercial Team in AI Search Behavior

Know how results are generated.

Know how AI weights content.

Know how to build presence across voice, chat, and image-based discovery.

2️⃣ Run AI Visibility Audits Weekly

Ask Google’s AI what the best hotels are in your area.

Ask ChatGPT.

Ask Perplexity.

Map out where your hotel appears and where it vanishes.

3️⃣ Build visibility where it counts.

Build Campaigns Designed for AI Input

Use AI-generated images, voice agents, and content trained on structured prompts.

Don’t rely on manual copy.

Don’t wait on creative teams.

Push content daily.

4️⃣ Study How Google Labels and Ranks Sponsored AI Content

Track how the HVAC ads are placed.

Learn what types of queries trigger them.

Understand where Google is testing.

Follow their roadmap.

Then get ahead of it.

5️⃣ Build Booking Paths That Bypass the AI Gate

Use AI tools on your own site.

Create direct voice agents.

Generate content designed to capture guest interest before they ever hit Google.

This is a shift.

Google is laying the foundation for AI monetization across every vertical.

HVAC today.

Hotels tomorrow.

If your team isn’t ready, the direct channel won’t just shrink.

It will disappear from the results entirely.
