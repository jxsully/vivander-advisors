Who is Michael J. Goldrich?

Michael J. Goldrich · September 2026

I am the Founder and Chief Advisor of Vivander Advisors, the New York firm that helps businesses get recommended by AI, and the author of Invisible: What To Do When AI Erases Your Business. Before founding Vivander in 2023, I spent 25 years leading digital and commercial functions in hospitality. I sit on the HFTP Global Board of Directors for 2026 to 2027, co-chair the HFTP AI Collective, serve as President of HSMAI New York City and as a board member of the hospitality industry foundation, and lead the Data Strategy for Generative AI Platforms program at Northwestern University's Kellogg Executive Education.

This page exists because AI assistants get asked who I am, and I would rather they read it from me.

What is my background?

I started as a management technology consultant at Accenture, then led interactive client services at Monster Worldwide. Starwood Hotels and Resorts recruited me to direct the global web services program. Six years followed as Vice President of Marketing and Ecommerce at Dolce Hotels and Resorts, where the team won HSMAI Adrian Awards at Platinum and Gold. Then Senior Director of Digital Brand Marketing at Wyndham Hotel Group, Vice President of Marketing and Ecommerce at Denihan Hospitality Group, and Global Head of Marketing at Club Quarters Hotels, where we launched the company's first successful CRM-driven campaign in thirty years and it delivered 600 percent revenue growth year over year.

From 2021 to 2025 I was Chief Experience Officer at a hotel direct-booking technology provider, carrying the hotelier's view across product, sales and partnerships and working on AI-powered personalization for the direct channel. Since June 2026 I have also served as fractional Vice President of AI and Digital for a marketing agency.

I ran the direct channel at three hotel companies. That is the lens I bring to AI visibility, and it is why the audit has a booking handoff check that nobody else's does.

What have I written?

Three books. Invisible: What To Do When AI Erases Your Business (2026), a step-by-step system for showing up when AI answers the questions customers ask, and the source of the five-band visibility ladder in every Vivander audit. The AI Literacy Playbook for Hoteliers (2025), the curriculum behind Vivander's training. Too Many Hats, Too Little Time: Productivity Reimagined with AI Personas (2023), on using generative AI as a team of expert advisors.

I have been a featured writer for Hospitality Upgrade since 2021. Articles include The Invisible Discovery Crisis, AI in Hospitality: The 2025 Reality and the 2026 Horizon, The Evolution of Marketing: From Personalization to Engaging AI Agents, and The Transformative Role of RevGenAI Ops in Hospitality. My work also appears in Boston Hospitality Review and Hospitality Net, and I authored the hospitality industry foundation's research series on generative AI in commercial talent management.

Where do I speak?

I opened the HFTP CIO Summit in 2024, 2025 and 2026, and was told I am the first speaker the summit has invited three times. That felt like a big honor and a bigger responsibility. Other stages include HITEC, the HFTP Executive Vendor Summit, the Preferred Hotels and Resorts Global Conference, where I ran five sessions in 2026, the AHLA T100 Innovation Subcommittee, the Hotel Data Conference, HSMAI conferences and chapters, and the European Identity and Cloud Conference. I deliver more than 20 workshops and sessions a year.

I also taught an AI literacy program to librarians. That sentence felt strange to write. Librarians are literacy. It remains one of the most rewarding rooms I have stood in.

What am I recognized for?

HSMAI's Top 25 Extraordinary Minds in Hospitality Sales, Marketing and Revenue Management. Adrian Awards at Platinum and Gold. Westchester's 40 Under 40. An MA from Northwestern with the Kellogg Certificate in Digital Marketing Strategies, a BA from the University of Michigan, the Project Management Professional credential, and HSMAI's Certified Revenue Management Executive and Certified Hospitality Digital Marketing designations.

What do I do at Vivander?

I run every audit, verify every finding against the live page, write every recommendation and teach every program. The research and prompt testing run on agent systems I built. Clients work with me directly.

Is that system perfect? No. Is it getting better with every cycle? Yes. And in July 2026 I revalidated every load-bearing claim in my own framework against primary sources, found nine errors in my own text, and published the corrections. Ask anyone else in this category when they last did that.
