Your Direct Channel Is Now Competing With an AI That Plans Trips

Michael J. Goldrich · November 25, 2025

What Is Truly New About Gemini 3

Earlier AI answered questions.
Gemini 3 understands goals.

Earlier AI generated text.
Gemini 3 completes tasks.

Earlier AI suggested options.
Gemini 3 decides.

This model can read documents, watch videos, analyze data, plan complex workflows, execute actions, and handle multi-step tasks that used to require teams of real people.

A guest can say:

“Plan a quick trip. I want a boutique hotel with late checkout and a pool.
Book me something that fits my taste.”

Gemini chooses, books, and completes the workflow.

Your website never enters the picture unless the AI decides it needs to.

This is the first time in hospitality history that the guest journey can be fully completed without the guest ever touching a hotel-controlled channel.

That is the shift.

If your content is unclear, unstructured, outdated, or irrelevant, Gemini 3 simply removes you from consideration.

If you are not legible to the machine, you are invisible to the human.

Hotels are still thinking in channels.

Gemini 3 thinks in outcomes.

What Commercial Teams Must Do Now

You need to rethink how your team works, how your content is structured (schema), and how your direct channel communicates with systems that filter guest decisions.

5 Immediate Shifts to Stay Visible and Bookable

1️⃣ Create content that machines can understand first

Structure your offers, amenities, location details, and value props so the AI can instantly identify relevance.

2️⃣ Have your team use AI every day until it becomes instinctive

Prompts are the new skill set.

The better your team can guide AI, the faster you can produce campaigns, offers, scripts, and content that win with guests and with machines.

3️⃣ Transform your website into an AI-ready booking layer

Your direct channel must integrate with AI voice agents, search agents, and intelligent workflows that guide guests from question to confirmation.

4️⃣ Move all first-touch guest communication to AI Voice Agents

Calls, chats, and general inquiries should be answered instantly, in multiple languages, by AI agents that convert.

Staff should handle exceptions, not volume.

5️⃣ Adopt an AI operating system for commercial execution

You set the intent.

AI handles the campaign build, the copy, the visuals, the rollout, and the reporting.

You focus on strategy.

The system handles production.

The New Reality

Gemini 3 decides before the guest decides.

Gemini filters before the guest filters.

Gemini guides the journey before your website ever loads.

If your hotel cannot communicate clearly to this new layer of intelligence, you will not be presented as an option.

This is not a future problem.

Gemini 3 is already active.
