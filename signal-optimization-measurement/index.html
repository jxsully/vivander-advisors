More AI Mentions? Measure What Changed Before Calling It Progress.

Michael J. Goldrich · October 19, 2026

A business appears in more AI answers this month. The dashboard looks better. The team wants to know which changes worked.

That is a reasonable question. A higher mention count alone cannot answer it.

Perhaps the business is appearing for more relevant buying questions. Perhaps the question mix changed. Perhaps the assistant is repeating an outdated capability. These situations can produce similar totals and require different decisions.

Measurement makes signal optimization useful when it connects an observed answer to a question the business can investigate.

Keep mentions, recommendations and citations separate

A mention means the answer names the business. A recommendation means the answer presents the business as a suitable choice. A citation is a link to a supporting source. The source may belong to the business or to somebody else.

These outcomes can overlap, but they do not always move together. An educational article may receive a citation without a recommendation to hire its author. A hotel may receive a recommendation while a travel publication receives the source link.

Being retrieved is another distinct outcome. Unless a platform exposes its retrieval activity, a business generally cannot establish every source considered from the final answer alone. Label unobserved retrieval as unknown.

Discover mentions before checking facts

A monitoring process should detect the business wherever it appears in the sampled answers. Fact checking comes afterward.

Suppose a business appears in 18 of 50 completed answer runs. Four of those answers contain an incorrect service claim. All 18 mentions still belong in the mention count. The four errors belong in a separate accuracy record.

This hypothetical example shows why a fact reference should not become a filter that hides mentions. Filtering the errors out of visibility reporting would remove some of the most useful findings.

Maintain a verified business fact record for the accuracy check. When a claim is outside that record, mark it for investigation. An unknown claim is not automatically false.

Make the baseline reproducible

Use questions drawn from actual customer decisions. Include relevant category searches, specific needs, comparisons and brand questions. Keep these groups identifiable rather than blending everything into one score.

For each observation, retain the exact question, date, platform, available model or product setting, location context, search setting, answer text and cited links. Record whether the run completed successfully. Keep consumer-interface tests separate from API tests unless comparability has been established.

OpenAI explains that ChatGPT search can rewrite questions and use location or relevant memory. Those documented behaviors are reasons to record context. They also explain why two people asking similar questions may not be running the same test.

Repeat observations under consistent conditions. A single answer is an example, rather than a stable estimate of how a platform always behaves.

Keep the denominator visible

“Mention rate increased” needs a definition. State whether the calculation uses completed answer runs, distinct questions or another unit. Keep failed runs visible in a separate reliability count.

If the question set grows, report the original question cohort separately from the new questions. Otherwise, an apparent change may reflect the sample rather than a change in representation.

Also separate branded and unbranded questions. An answer naming a business after being asked about it measures something different from the business appearing in an open category recommendation.

A practical reporting view includes mention rate, recommendation rate, own-site and third-party citations, and material factual errors. Add business outcomes, such as qualified inquiries, where the available analytics support them.

Use a change log to frame the investigation

Record interventions with dates and evidence: a corrected listing, a revised service page, a published case study or a repaired crawl issue. Name the owner and the questions the change was intended to help.

When later answers change, compare them with that log. Open the cited pages and check whether they support the new statements. If the same error persists, look for remaining contradictions or missing information.

A comparison group of unchanged questions can provide context. It still cannot control every platform update, source change or variation in generated answers. Describe the result as an observed association unless the study design supports a stronger conclusion.

Know what monitoring can establish

Monitoring can document answers, mentions, recommendations, citations and changes within the sample. It can identify a source worth examining. It cannot reveal every reason an AI system selected a business.

OpenAI itself notes that search citations can be incomplete or incorrect and recommends checking the cited source. Treat a citation as evidence to inspect rather than a complete explanation.

Vivander’s audit examines representation, business facts and relevant sources to identify practical corrections. Ongoing monitoring tracks sampled answers over time. Further source inspection or technical checks may be needed to investigate a change.

A report becomes useful when it ends with an action: verify a disputed claim, correct a source, investigate a new citation or continue observing a pattern that is still uncertain.
