What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

Michael J. Goldrich · September 2026

Generative engine optimization is the work of influencing how generative AI models represent a brand when a user asks an open-ended question and the model assembles its answer from many sources. SEO is about being found. AEO is about being extractable. GEO is about being selected: corroborated by the sources the platform trusts, resolved as a clear entity, vouched for by third parties. Most of GEO happens off your own site. At Vivander Advisors I measure it as the off-site layer of the AI Visibility Audit, and I handle the term with care, because the field’s best-known GEO claim is one I refuse to make.

Repetition rules. Third-party mentions create the consensus the engines copy. That is GEO in two sentences, and the second one is where the money goes.

What does the evidence say GEO is?

The clearest causal demonstration is Ahrefs’ 2025 experiment with a brand that did not exist. Three fabricated off-site pages, seeded against an official on-site FAQ, flipped five of eight AI products into reciting the fabrications within two months. Specific fiction beat vague truth. One brand, no control, vendor-run. Evidence for the mechanism. None for a magnitude.

The correlations point the same way. Across 75,000 brands, branded web mentions correlated with AI visibility at 0.664 on ChatGPT and 0.709 on AI Mode, YouTube mentions at roughly 0.737, backlinks at 0.218. Mentions outpredict links. The multiple varies by study, so I never quote one.

What is the GEO claim to avoid?

“The Princeton GEO paper shows a 40 percent uplift.”

The 2024 study by Princeton, IIT Delhi and Georgia Tech found that citing sources, quotations and statistics raised visibility while an authoritative tone did not. Those findings hold. The headline number does not travel. It comes from a fifth-ranked source, conditional on already being retrieved, on a 2023 model, with scores normalized to sum to one so the first source lost 30 percent by construction. The source was pre-injected into context. Retrieval was never tested.

As a general claim it is banned in my evidence register. Two more sit beside it. Only 3 of 54 method-and-domain combinations in a 2025 benchmark of GEO techniques were significantly positive, and none in question answering. And a 2026 study found body-only GEO optimization reduced retrieval and citation, because nobody checked whether the page was retrieved first.

How does GEO timing work?

Two halves on two clocks. The retrieval half is live: a new corroborating source is read the next time the platform fetches it, and it moves in days to weeks. Its training half is slow: what a model learned before its cutoff sits in the weights until the next version. Treat the training half as reputation infrastructure and the retrieval half as the tactical lever.

What does GEO involve in practice?

Finding which sources each platform cites for the category and market. Making the facts on those sources match the site. Earning specific, dated mentions on the two or three that matter rather than volume on outlets the platform never reads. Adding video with a transcript. Resolving name collisions and aliases. Refusing self-published rankings, which work briefly and decay.

How does Vivander measure GEO?

As the off-site layer. A seven-category evidence sweep of what third-party material exists. Then a review-surface sweep against named competitors. A press and publications landscape. An entity-layer check. And the citation data itself, per platform. Cross-source consistency is weighted on a stated rationale and flagged as gated on my own data, because no published study measures it, and I would rather tell you that than dress it up.

Where this is going

The engines are not going to stop reading other people’s pages about you. They are going to read more of them, faster, and cross-check them against each other. GEO is the discipline of making sure the pages they find agree, and say something specific.

Consensus is the new authority. You do not get to declare it. What you get is to earn it, three sources at a time.
