How can hotels and restaurants prepare for AI agents?

Hotels and restaurants can prepare for AI agents by making their offers easy to verify and their booking paths reliable. Publish clear policies, keep menus and property details consistent, connect transactions to current inventory, and preserve human escalation. Test these foundations before investing in autonomous negotiation or payment features.This guide applies Katja Forbes’s retailer chapter in Mastercard’s A Short History of the Future of Shopping and Payments to hospitality operations. Her scenarios include agents checking claims, negotiating value and carrying customer preferences. For hotel and restaurant leaders, the immediate task is to make the offer trustworthy enough to evaluate.A guest’s assistant is another route through which the guest may research your organization. Loyalty, service standards and commercial discipline still matter. Treat the person behind the request with the same care you would offer through a concierge or reservation agent.

Why clear hospitality information is a commercial asset

OpenTable’s 2025 US consumer study found that 54% researched restaurants before booking; those who did spent around 21 minutes on that research. Its Concierge announcement described menus, reviews and restaurant descriptions as inputs for AI-supported answers. The evidence connects discovery to information quality, although it does not establish a guaranteed booking increase. [2]The National Restaurant Association’s 2024 technology report, based on more than 1,000 adult consumers, identified convenience-enhancing ordering and payment options as priorities. Among off-premises consumers, 84% said they would order through a restaurant website. Stated willingness is not measured transaction volume, and preferences vary by service segment. [3]Vivander’s operational conclusion: reliable information and a usable direct path are investments with value today, whether the visitor is browsing personally or using an assistant.

Hotel AI readiness: make room offers comparable

A hotel offer is a combination of room, dates, occupancy, inclusions and restrictions. Publishing the lowest headline price without its conditions makes comparisons unreliable. Describe the offer precisely and let the booking engine confirm the transaction-specific details.

Separate room facts from rate-plan rules

Room size, bed configuration and accessible features belong with the room. Breakfast, refundability, deposit terms and loyalty eligibility belong with the rate. Keep requested amenities distinct from guaranteed ones. This structure helps a guest or assistant compare like with like.

Protect the direct booking proposition

Explain genuine direct benefits, such as a defined cancellation option or included service, with their conditions. Avoid unsupported claims that booking direct always produces the lowest price. Compare net contribution after acquisition costs, cancellations and service delivery, rather than chasing booking volume alone.

Hotel example: value without a blanket discount

Illustrative scenario: an assistant compares a two-night stay that includes parking with a cheaper room-only rate. The hotel wins when its total and inclusions fit the guest’s needs. If offering an additional benefit, apply approved inventory and margin rules. A conversational counteroffer should never silently override rate restrictions.

Restaurant AI readiness: make menus and reservations usable

A restaurant needs a current text menu, accurate opening hours and a reservation path that reflects its real service. Photos help diners understand the setting. Text helps them inspect the options, prices and conditions.
  • Menus: distinguish lunch, dinner, tasting and seasonal menus; date changes; list prices and relevant service-charge information.
  • Reservations: explain party-size limits, deposits, seating duration and cancellation rules before confirmation.
  • Special needs: publish accessible features precisely and route dietary questions to trained staff.
  • Orders: clarify collection times, delivery area, customization options and sold-out items through the ordering system.
  • Group dining: separate ordinary table bookings from private events and minimum-spend commitments.
Illustrative scenario: a group asks for an early dinner before a theater performance. A useful response combines opening time, location, a realistic meal format and reservation availability. “Ideal for every occasion” does little to resolve the actual decision.

How to make hotel and restaurant claims verifiable

Attach specific evidence to the claims that influence selection. A sustainability statement should explain the practice and, where relevant, identify the certification and its scope. An accessibility statement should identify actual features. Do not infer a service guarantee from a marketing adjective.
Weak claim More useful evidence Responsible owner
Family-friendly hotel Room occupancy, bed arrangements, child policies and relevant facilities. Rooms and reservations team.
Accessible venue Entrance, elevator, restroom and room features; contact for individual requirements. Property operations.
Sustainable dining Defined sourcing or waste practices, current certification and scope. Food and beverage leadership.
Flexible booking Exact cancellation deadline, applicable rate or reservation and refund process. Revenue and reservation teams.
Forbes’s report explores much broader verification and negotiation by 2030. Those are scenarios to test. They are not evidence that every current assistant can verify a property’s claims, or that product-passport rules automatically apply to hotels and restaurants. [1]

SEO and AEO for hotels and restaurants: what to fix first

Start with clear, indexable pages that answer real guest questions. Give each page a descriptive title, a logical heading structure, accurate local details and relevant images. Link related information so the visitor can move from an answer to the offer and its booking route.Use appropriate Hotel or Restaurant structured data on the business pages, matching visible facts. Keep date-specific inventory in the transaction system. Schema can describe the business; it cannot repair incorrect content or guarantee an AI recommendation.Google’s guidance says no additional technical requirements or special schema are necessary for AI Overviews or AI Mode. Important content should be available as text, and structured data should agree with the page. [4]

Test the path from answer to action

Check whether a visitor can identify the correct property or venue, understand its restrictions and reach the booking engine. If an integration supports agent actions, test authentication, stale inventory, retries and confirmations in the provider’s supported test environment. Keep existing security protections in place.

A 90-day AI readiness plan for hospitality operators

  1. Days 1–30: establish reliable facts. Inventory the questions that influence bookings, assign content owners and reconcile your organization’s website and listings.
  2. Days 31–60: strengthen the booking handoff. Review live pricing, inventory, deposits and cancellation visibility with reservation, ordering and technology partners.
  3. Days 61–90: run a bounded pilot. Test a defined set of guest scenarios, log failures and compare results against your baseline.
Track factual accuracy, successful handoffs, booking conversion where measurable, net contribution and unresolved service issues. Set a review date and a stop condition before expanding the pilot.The operational advantage comes from making a real promise easier to inspect and easier to fulfill. Hotel and restaurant AI readiness: frequently asked questions

What makes a hotel or restaurant ready for AI agents? Reliable facts, comparable offers, current transaction data, visible restrictions and a usable booking path provide the foundation. A team should own the information and test complete guest scenarios. Advanced agent integrations are a separate provider-dependent investment.
Do hospitality businesses need a chatbot for agentic commerce? A chatbot is not a prerequisite for accurate public information or a reliable reservation path. Prioritize the problems guests face. Add a conversational feature when it has a defined purpose, maintained information and a human escalation route.
Does structured data guarantee an AI recommendation? No. Structured data should accurately describe visible content, but it cannot guarantee indexing, ranking or recommendation. Google states that its AI search features do not require special schema or additional technical requirements beyond normal search eligibility.
