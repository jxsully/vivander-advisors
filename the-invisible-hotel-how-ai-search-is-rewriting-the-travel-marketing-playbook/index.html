The Invisible Hotel: How AI Search Is Rewriting the Travel Marketing Playbook

Michael J. Goldrich · July 15, 2025

Something profound is happening in travel search and most hotel marketers aren’t ready for it.

Generative AI is no longer a novelty. According to recent research, 80 percent of consumers booking flights and hotels now use AI-driven platforms to guide their purchasing decisions. Tools like Google’s AI Overviews, Expedia’s Romie, and ChatGPT plugins are changing the way travelers search, plan, and book.

And yet, most hotel marketing strategies remain stuck in a world where search engine marketing and traditional SEO dominate.

Here’s the brutal truth. Your hotel may already be invisible.

In early 2025, visibility for hotel-related keywords in Google AI Overviews rose fivefold. But who’s benefiting? Just 20 websites dominate 75 percent of AI Overview results for flight queries. For hotels, the situation is worse. Only a single direct hotel brand ranks among the top 20. The rest of the visibility is consumed by online travel agencies, user-generated content platforms like Reddit and TripAdvisor, and AI-curated Wikipedia entries.

This is no longer a future threat. It is a present reality.

Why Hotels Are Losing the AI Search War

AI tools prioritize content that feels human, helpful, and community-driven. AI overviews consistently favor user-generated content, independent reviews, and forum content. Corporate marketing copy and even paid ads are often pushed below the fold or omitted entirely.

The implications are staggering.

Your SEO content strategy may be obsolete.

Your paid ads are now competing for even less visible real estate.

Your direct channel may already be bypassed.

Hotels that once fought and won the direct booking wars against online travel agencies are now being outmaneuvered by AI algorithms that treat OTAs and forums as the authoritative source.

How to Reclaim Your Visibility and Your Future

Here’s how hotels and travel brands can respond starting today.

Precision Targeting Must Become Non-Negotiable

Broad targeting is dead. Hotels must shift to data-driven precision targeting, combining first-party data with strategic partnerships to reach potential travelers before AI curates their options away. Focus on signals, not demographics. If you don’t have real-time insights into what your audience is searching, comparing, and booking, you are behind.

Don’t Abandon Awareness. Double Down on It

AI tools might influence search behavior, but travelers still consume content. U.S. consumers view an average of 185 pages of travel content in the 45 days before booking.

This is not the time to shrink your upper funnel. Use connected TV, online video, display, and native advertising to inject your brand story early into travelers’ minds. Remind them why booking direct matters long before AI tools suggest otherwise.

The lesson from past direct booking wars remains true. Brand campaigns work. But now they need to work harder across more fragmented digital landscapes.

Create Human, Shareable Content AI Cannot Ignore

AI is changing the how but not the why. Travelers are still human, indecisive, curious, and emotional. Their journeys are long, messy, and nonlinear.

You must create content that AI ranks because humans trust it. Invest in authentic storytelling, real guest experiences, and community engagement. Leverage your past guests. Build forums. Feature real staff. Encourage and curate reviews.

In short, design your content to appeal to people first, not algorithms. Ironically, that is what today’s AI prefers.

Optimize for Owned Channels

AI will not push your direct website unless you force the issue. Optimize your site structure for AI discoverability using schema markup and structured content. Deploy AI-powered chatbots and recommendation tools on your own site to compete with AI agents elsewhere. Shift focus from traffic acquisition to conversion optimization on your owned platforms.

The Bottom Line. Adapt or Disappear

The emergence of generative AI in travel search is not a temporary disruption. It is a structural shift.

If your hotel’s strategy still revolves around optimizing Google Search Console reports and managing pay-per-click bids, you are missing the real battle. It is no longer about keyword rankings. It is about becoming the most helpful, most human, and most memorable source so that when AI tools surface answers, your brand is not absent from the conversation.

The era of invisible hotels has begun.

Do not let yours be one of them.
