Book one

Invisible. What to do when AI erases your business.

The most consequential shift in how customers find businesses since search — and almost no one is ready for it.

ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude are quietly rewriting which businesses customers consider. Whole categories are losing share of recommendation without realizing it — because the thing that replaced Google is answering in complete sentences, not ten blue links.

Invisible is a working playbook for the companies that refuse to disappear: how answer engines decide what to recommend, how to audit your current footprint, and the specific moves that restore visibility where it matters.
