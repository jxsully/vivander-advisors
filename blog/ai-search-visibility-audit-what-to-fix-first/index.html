AI recommends competitors or gets your business wrong. How can Vivander help?

Michael J. Goldrich · September 3, 2026

Vivander Answers · 3 of 10

By Michael J. Goldrich, Founder and Chief Advisor, Vivander Advisors.

I begin by checking the actual buyer question, the answer and the sources behind it. Vivander’s visibility audit can identify missing information, inaccurate facts and relevant competitors, then prioritize changes your team can make. We can improve evidence about your business; we cannot control or guarantee an AI platform’s recommendations.

What does “invisible to AI” mean in practice?

It could mean your business is absent from relevant recommendations, mentioned without a supporting citation or described incorrectly. Those are separate problems. A company can appear in an answer and still be a poor match for the buyer’s question.

I want the question to reflect a real purchase decision. “What is our company?” differs from “Who can help a hospitality team develop and roll out an AI strategy?” The second includes a service and context that the answer must resolve.

What evidence should we collect?

Save the question, platform, date, answer and accessible sources. Include the facts you expected to see and why they are relevant. Repeated observations help reveal variability, but one answer should not become a permanent verdict on the business.

The Vivander Visibility Audit examines buyer questions and the evidence behind answers. Its purpose is to turn uncertainty into priorities, not to infer every platform’s internal ranking mechanism.

What can your team actually change?

We review whether your site clearly explains services, audience, location, qualifications and relevant constraints. We examine whether important pages can be reached and read, and whether outside business descriptions agree. An outdated directory entry may require a different owner from an unclear service page.

In Vivander’s published hospitality audit, five of six platforms repeated a dogs-and-cats policy. The verified brand overview specified dogs only, while the property FAQ did not name the permitted species. The recommended fix was to state the verified policy clearly and reconcile conflicting sources. A capability or policy should never be invented to make a business look attractive. Read the anonymized hospitality case and its evidence.

How do you prioritize fixes?

I look at buyer relevance, evidence, dependencies and what your team can implement. An incorrect service description on a page you control may be an immediate correction. A third-party source may require an update request. A missing topic may need a substantial answer page.

Each recommendation should explain the problem, action and responsible owner. Changes must fit what you do. Publishing near-identical pages or adding claims only to structured data creates another problem: the information stops being trustworthy.

Which business facts should we verify first?

Start with the offer customers can actually buy, who it serves and the constraints that affect fit. Confirm current contact details, service areas and any qualifications you choose to publish. Then check whether important pages and external descriptions agree. If a product is still being tested, say so. If a service requires a scoped proposal, explain the process. The strongest answer is one your team can verify and stand behind when a prospect calls.

How do we know whether it helped?

We can retest agreed questions and compare answers and sources over time. Website analytics and inquiry records provide separate evidence about visits and outcomes. They do not automatically prove that a particular AI answer caused a sale.

Review Visibility Monitoring if you need a recurring record after the initial work. The objective is relevant, accurate discovery and learning what changed, with measurement limits visible.

Related questions

Can Vivander guarantee a ChatGPT recommendation?

No. AI platforms control their answers. Vivander can review evidence, improve accurate business information and measure observed changes within a defined scope.

Can you remove competitors from AI answers?

Vivander cannot control an AI platform’s answers. We assess whether your business is a relevant fit and which supporting information is missing or inaccurate.

Is adding keywords enough?

No. Keywords cannot substitute for verified services, useful answers, accessible pages and consistent business descriptions.
