A client meeting ended. Where does the next promise live?

Michael J. Goldrich · September 13, 2026

MyTeam · Article

MyTeam brings clients, projects and next actions into a personal workflow, with an AI coach and prepared work for review. Opening soon.

By Michael J. Goldrich, Vivander Advisors.

The next promise should live beside the client or project it belongs to, with a clear action you can review. MyTeam is designed to bring those records into a personal work system alongside a daily coach and AI teammates. It is opening soon.

A consultant can leave a productive call with a scattered record: notes in one place, an email draft in another, a task on a separate list. By Friday, the client is waiting for an answer and the owner has to reconstruct what was agreed.

What belongs in the client record?

Keep the context needed for the next action: what was discussed, what was agreed, what remains open and what you owe next. Specific wording helps. “Follow up with client” gives little direction. “Confirm the revised workshop scope and send the agenda” tells you what preparation is needed.

MyTeam’s clients and projects section is described as keeping each engagement’s actions and notes together. Its sales section covers the people you are pursuing and what you owe them next. Those are related records with different stages of work.

How can the team help with preparation?

A writer can prepare a follow-up draft. A researcher can organize relevant findings. An analyst can help summarize the material supplied. The output waits for your review, including any facts the teammate could not confirm.

For a fictional advisory engagement, you might use meeting notes to prepare an agenda and a summary of open questions. You check that the draft reflects the agreed scope before approving it. The record should distinguish what the client agreed from what you are still proposing.

How does the coach change the daily review?

A client promise can become a morning commitment. At night, the coach compares that intention with the work recorded. A completed draft and a sent follow-up represent different stages. Seeing the difference helps you identify whether preparation or final review is where the work keeps stopping.

The coach’s reading depends on the available record. Keep actions and notes current enough to support an honest review. The next day can then start with the actual outstanding promise.

Which sections do you need?

The section guide describes tools for clients, projects, sales, events, travel and money. The Business plan includes these alongside the coach and team. Switch on the sections that fit your work, and review the current plan details and launch availability.

MyTeam is designed for one person running their own work. If that describes you, start by identifying the promise you most often lose track of. A system earns its place when that next action becomes easier to find, prepare and finish.
