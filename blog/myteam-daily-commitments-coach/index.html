Compare morning commitments with the work recorded during the day.

Michael J. Goldrich · September 17, 2026

MyTeam · Article

MyTeam pairs morning commitments with an evening review of the work recorded during your day. The product is opening soon.

By Michael J. Goldrich, Vivander Advisors.

MyTeam is designed to compare the commitments you make in the morning with the evidence recorded during your day. Its coach gives you an evening reading, while an AI team prepares work for your review. MyTeam is opening soon.

A long task list tells you what remains. A short commitment list establishes what you meant to complete today. That distinction gives the evening review a concrete question: did the work you said mattered receive the attention you planned?

What does a useful morning commitment look like?

Make it specific enough to assess. “Move the proposal forward” can mean almost anything. “Send the revised proposal after checking the scope” identifies an outcome. Pick a few commitments that fit the day you actually have, including meetings and existing obligations.

MyTeam’s morning workflow starts with that short list. The purpose is to give the day a clear set of intentions that can be compared with what follows.

What does the coach read at night?

The coach is described as reading evidence such as time logged, meetings held, mail sent and work closed. It uses that record to assess commitments. The value of the review depends on the quality and completeness of the information available.

Imagine a fictional consultant who committed to sending a proposal, finishing an analysis and calling a supplier. The proposal went out. The analysis was started. The call has no recorded activity. Those observations give the consultant something specific to examine before planning tomorrow.

Where does the team fit?

During the day, the team can prepare drafts, research and summaries. A writer can help with a proposal draft. A researcher can compare supplier information. You review the result and approve it before it goes out.

Preparation and completion remain distinct. A ready draft may move a commitment forward. The proposal still needs your review and the decision to send it.

Which MyTeam features fit the user’s needs?

MyTeam lets you switch on the sections you want. Some people may start with the coach; others may need clients, projects or sales alongside the daily workflow. The sections page explains which tools belong to each plan.

The product is built around one person managing their own work. Its launch page limits availability to adults in the United States. Read the current availability and first-month expectations before planning to use it.

The starting habit needs no elaborate setup: write three commitments tomorrow morning, then inspect what actually happened that evening. MyTeam is being built to support that daily discipline.
