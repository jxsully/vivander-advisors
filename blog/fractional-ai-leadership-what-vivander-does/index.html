How does Vivander’s fractional AI leadership support your organization?

Michael J. Goldrich · September 21, 2026

Vivander Answers · 2 of 10

By Michael J. Goldrich, Founder and Chief Advisor, Vivander Advisors.

Vivander works alongside your organization’s existing team to turn business priorities into an AI strategy and a practical rollout. Fractional AI leadership can include choosing use cases, coordinating pilots, clarifying ownership, building employee fluency and reviewing progress. Vivander agrees the responsibilities, time commitment and deliverables before the engagement begins.

What changes when leadership is fractional?

Fractional means ongoing, part-time involvement rather than a full-time executive hire. It gives the company access to leadership for a defined need and scope. It does not automatically give me authority over every function or make Vivander your IT department. Those boundaries need to be explicit.

The value is connecting decisions that otherwise sit in separate meetings: which problem matters, which workflow should change, which tools are suitable, who approves use and how employees learn. I help those decisions become a plan people can execute.

What would Vivander works on first?

Vivander starts with your organization’s priorities, existing experiments and the work people actually perform. Vivander identifies where AI could improve quality, effort, customer experience or commercial execution. Then Vivander compares opportunities against constraints such as data access, review requirements, capacity and dependencies.

A fictional sales team might want faster proposal preparation. Vivander would define approved source material, the expected output, human review and baseline effort. That is more actionable than asking everyone to use a new tool without a shared task.

Who owns the pilot and rollout?

Your organization needs a sponsor with authority to make business decisions and a workflow owner who understands the work. IT, security, legal or other specialists participate where their remit applies. Vivander can coordinate the plan and support execution within the agreed responsibilities.

Vivander documents who selects tools, approves data access, reviews outputs, supports employees and decides whether a pilot expands. Your organization retains its business decisions. If a vendor must configure an integration, Vivander includes that dependency rather than assuming advisory work supplies every technical resource.

How should your organization decide whether to expand a pilot?

Vivander agrees what success looks like before the pilot. Measures can include output quality, total effort including review and correction, repeat use, exceptions and owner confidence. The measures depend on the task. A polished demonstration is not enough evidence for a company rollout.

When results are mixed, the next step may be to revise the workflow, change training, narrow the use case or stop. My company AI rollout guide explains that decision process.

What would the working rhythm look like?

Vivander and your organization should agree the meeting cadence, which decisions need review and how the team raises blockers. A useful working session can examine a pilot result, confirm the next action and assign an owner. Between sessions, people need a place to record issues and decisions. The cadence depends on scope and team capacity; fractional involvement is not an assumption of unlimited availability. Vivander defines access and expected participation so the company knows how to use the engagement.

What should remain after the engagement?

Your organization should know what it has decided, which workflows operate, who owns them and what needs attention. Depending on scope, deliverables can include a prioritized roadmap, pilot plans, decision records, rollout guidance and learning materials. Vivander specifies these in the proposal.

Handover matters from the beginning. I want your organization’s team to understand the reasoning and continue the work. Review fractional services, then bring a priority and your organization’s team structure to a discussion.

Related questions

Does fractional leadership replace your organization’s managers?

No. Vivander can work alongside your managers. Reporting lines, authority and responsibilities are agreed for the engagement.

Will Vivander build every integration?

Technical implementation is scoped where needed. Vivander identifies the responsible internal team or vendor rather than assuming every integration is included.

Is this only for mature AI programs?

The scope can address an early strategy or an existing program that needs coordination. Business needs, capacity and responsibilities determine fit.
