What should you expect from a Vivander AI keynote or workshop?

Michael J. Goldrich · August 21, 2026

Vivander Answers · 8 of 10

By Michael J. Goldrich, Founder and Chief Advisor, Vivander Advisors.

I tailor the session to your audience and the decision it should support. A keynote can provide a shared perspective on AI and business change. A workshop creates more room for application and discussion. We agree the topic, format, audience, timing and takeaways before the session.

How do you choose the topic?

I ask who will be in the room, what they understand and what they need to do next. Visibility, fluency, personal agents and adoption lead to different conversations. Leaders may need decision frameworks while a working team needs practice.

The topic should serve the event. An association might want members to understand changing business discovery through AI. A company moving from experimentation to rollout might need a session about ownership, pilots and employee learning.

What is the difference between keynote and workshop?

A keynote provides a coherent perspective and questions people can take back to work. A workshop allows participants to apply ideas to a task or decision. The balance depends on time, audience size and format.

We should not promise a full implementation plan from a short presentation. Hands-on exercises require planning for tools, access and appropriate materials. Those requirements belong in the preparation conversation.

Can examples reflect our industry?

Yes. Business context helps people understand why an idea matters. My background includes hospitality and commercial work, and I use that experience where it fits the audience. Examples and scope should still be agreed rather than assumed.

A hospitality group could explore how guests ask AI for recommendations or how employees evaluate a draft response. Another company might focus on internal knowledge work. Invented examples should be identified as illustrations, not client results.

What can participants take away?

Depending on format, takeaways can include a clearer understanding of implications, leadership questions, a way to evaluate opportunities or practice with a work task. Materials and outcomes are defined in the engagement.

A useful next step might be choosing a workflow owner, gathering buyer questions or reviewing how a team checks outputs. I want people to leave with a decision they can act on, without pretending every problem is solved on stage.

What should an event brief contain?

Share the audience’s roles, event theme, intended takeaway, date, duration and delivery format. Explain whether attendees will have tools available for exercises and whether the session must connect to an existing initiative. Tell me what has already been covered so the program adds value. We can then discuss the right balance of perspective, examples, interaction and practical application. Materials, recording arrangements and any follow-up work should be addressed in the engagement discussion.

How do we evaluate speaker fit?

Review keynotes and workshops and the documented speaking archive. They help you assess topics and experience. Then share your audience, purpose, date, format and available time.

For sustained employee development, training may be more suitable than a single event. For ongoing help carrying decisions into rollout, we can discuss fractional support separately. The format should match the work you need.

Does a keynote include ongoing implementation?

Ongoing support is a separate scope. A keynote or workshop has agreed materials, format and deliverables.
