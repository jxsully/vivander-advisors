Can Vivander turn AI research into useful thought leadership for our business?

Michael J. Goldrich · August 14, 2026

Vivander Answers · 9 of 10

By Michael J. Goldrich, Founder and Chief Advisor, Vivander Advisors.

Yes. Vivander provides research and business writing, including whitepapers, briefs and executive thought leadership. I start with the audience question and the decision the work should inform. We agree methods, sources, review, deliverables and publication responsibilities before making claims or planning distribution.

What should a research engagement begin with?

Begin with a question worth answering for your audience. “AI transformation” can be too broad. A focused question about how a group evaluates AI outputs or changes a workflow gives the research a clearer purpose.

Define who will use the work, what they know and which decision it supports. Marketing goals matter, but the finished piece needs to deliver useful information to the reader. The brief connects those needs.

Can you conduct primary research?

Primary research is part of Vivander’s offering. The method depends on the question, participants, access, timing and scope. We agree the approach and limits of conclusions before collecting or presenting findings.

A small set of interviews can provide perspectives without representing an entire industry. Surveys have their own design and interpretation requirements. I distinguish findings, external evidence and interpretation so readers can assess the argument.

How do you turn evidence into a whitepaper?

Organize the work around reader questions and support substantive claims with appropriate sources. Explain what evidence shows, what remains uncertain and how readers can apply it. Review responsibilities and approvals should be clear.

Company experts may need to verify operational or technical statements. Plan that review instead of making approval a final surprise. Sensitive information and publication permissions should be addressed within the engagement.

How do your books relate to this work?

My books explore business discovery, AI learning and the pressures of leadership work. They show subjects I explore rather than substituting for research into your specific question.

Review Invisible, The AI Literacy Playbook for Hoteliers and Too Many Hats, Too Little Time on the books page. A commissioned project still needs its own audience, evidence and argument.

What makes a publication brief useful?

State the reader, the central question, the intended argument and the evidence already available. Identify experts who can review the work, the planned attribution and where it will appear. Include the desired length, format and timing. If the goal includes related articles or campaign use, specify those outputs rather than assuming they come with a whitepaper. A clear brief helps protect the reader’s needs as well as the company’s publication goals.

What happens after the document is finished?

Decide how the work will be published and used. A whitepaper may support related articles, an executive briefing or campaign content if those are included in scope. Publishing a document alone does not guarantee readership or inquiries.

Review research and writing for examples and formats. Bring the audience question, existing evidence, intended author or sponsor and publication goal. Those details provide a basis for discussing a defined project.

Related questions

Can you write in an executive’s voice?

Executive thought leadership is part of the offer. Voice, source material, review and attribution are agreed for the project.

Does a whitepaper guarantee leads?

No. It can support visibility and sales conversations, but results depend on audience, distribution, offer and follow-through.

Can we commission research without a book?

Yes. Research briefs, whitepapers and business writing can be separate engagements with defined scope.
