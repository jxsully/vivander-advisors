A company changed leaders. Is there a sales conversation worth having?

Michael J. Goldrich · September 23, 2026

Sessions · Article

The Hidden Sales Leads track helps participants find public buying signals, check their relevance and prepare outreach for your review.

By Michael J. Goldrich, Vivander Advisors.

A leadership change can create a reason to investigate a company, provided the move is relevant to what you sell. The Find Hidden Sales Leads track in Agent Builder Sessions builds a system that watches those public signals, checks the source and prepares outreach you review.

The difficulty is the morning workload. A salesperson needs to read the territory, understand what changed, identify the right person and write something specific. That work is easy to defer when meetings and existing accounts fill the day.

Which signals does the system look for?

The sales track uses specialists for different signal families: new decision makers, funding, expansion, named initiatives, and rosters or shortlists. It scores findings around fit with what you sell and retains the evidence behind them.

Consider a fictional company opening a second office. The announcement establishes an expansion. Whether it creates demand for your service depends on your offer, location and the company’s circumstances. A useful finding explains that connection and identifies what remains uncertain.

How does it avoid turning news into a weak lead?

Your targets and qualification rules belong in the system’s memory and instructions. A broad instruction to find growing companies will return a broad list. A territory, a customer profile and the problem you solve give the researchers something concrete to evaluate.

The system includes a fact checker that opens the source page. The track also uses a ledger to avoid working the same account twice and a reservoir to retain surplus signals for quieter periods. Those are practical controls on a recurring research process.

What should the first draft say?

It should explain the reason for reaching out and connect it to a relevant offer. The sales track prepares outreach in your voice, opening with the company’s news. An inferred need should stay an inference. A funding announcement does not establish that a buyer has approved a budget for your service.

Contact information needs the same care. The system labels confidence in email guesses. You should verify an address and the recipient’s role before sending. A draft waiting in a folder gives you that decision point.

What do participants learn in the session?

You build the research, checking and drafting steps into a system on your laptop. The live session runs up to two hours with numbered pastes. You leave with files you can inspect and modify, and a schedule you set.

Review the Hidden Sales Leads track and the current formats. No account or conversation is guaranteed. The value is a repeatable way to investigate public changes and arrive at your morning review with more of the preparation done.
