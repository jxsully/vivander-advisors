What should an AI teammate finish before it asks for your approval?

Michael J. Goldrich · September 15, 2026

MyTeam · Article

MyTeam gives AI teammates defined roles and keeps drafts waiting for review, with uncertainties visible. The product is opening soon.

By Michael J. Goldrich, Vivander Advisors.

An AI teammate should prepare a reviewable piece of work, identify what it could not confirm, and make the next decision clear. MyTeam is designed around that handoff. Its team prepares the work, and you approve it before it is sent. The product is opening soon.

Delegation needs more than an instruction to help. A writer and a researcher serve different purposes. A draft email needs voice and context. A supplier comparison needs evidence, consistent criteria and clear gaps. Giving each teammate a defined role makes the output easier to assess.

Which roles does MyTeam describe?

The team page describes a chief of staff, writer, researcher and analyst, with experts you can add for your kind of work. They prepare drafts and findings for your review. When something cannot be confirmed, the teammate should tell you.

That admission is useful. A researcher comparing three supplier quotes may find that one leaves out the lead time. The comparison should preserve that gap, with a question to resolve before a purchasing decision.

What makes a handoff easy to review?

State the assignment, the material used, the prepared output and any remaining uncertainty. For a fictional client follow-up, the writer might prepare a message from meeting notes, flag an unconfirmed delivery date and leave it waiting for approval.

You should be able to decide whether to approve, revise or send it back with a note. An output that hides uncertainty forces you to repeat the investigation before trusting any part of it.

Which responsibilities stay with the user?

You assess whether the work is accurate and appropriate for the person receiving it. You verify consequential facts and decide what commitments to make. MyTeam’s approval step keeps that decision visible. Its team is designed to prepare work for you to review.

The same principle applies to research. A prepared summary can organize options and reveal missing information. It does not remove the need for judgment about the choice.

How does this connect with the daily coach?

The coach looks at the user’s commitments and what the record shows. The team helps with preparation between those two moments. Keeping both in one workflow can make it easier to see whether ready work became completed work.

MyTeam is built around an individual’s commitments, with sections that can be switched on as needed. Review the day-and-week walkthrough and launch status. A good first assignment is a recurring draft you already know how to judge. It gives you a clear basis for deciding whether the handoff helps.
