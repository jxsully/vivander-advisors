ChatGPT vs Perplexity vs Gemini vs Google AI Mode: which matters most for hotel discovery?

Michael J. Goldrich · September 2026

All of them, separately, because they share almost no sources. URL-level overlap between engines runs between 11 and 18 percent, and a 45-study review concluded there is no such thing as a global AI ranking. Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode reach the most travelers because they sit inside Google search. ChatGPT is where best-of questions get asked, and it cites Google Maps heavily for them. Perplexity and Gemini lean on Reddit in general and on OTAs for hotels. At Vivander Advisors I test six platforms and report each on its own, because averaging them hides the one your guests use.

A hotel can be recommended on Gemini and absent from ChatGPT for the same prompt, and both readings are correct. I see it every month.

The platforms, side by side

Sources: Peec AI (30 million sources, March 2026), Muck Rack (~25 million links, May 2026), Profound (680 million citations). Every share is dated because every share moves.

Which one reaches the most guests?

Google's. AI Overviews and AI Mode appear inside the results page most travelers still start on, and Google's AI queries run about three times longer than traditional ones. For a hotel, the Business Profile, YouTube presence and OTA listings feed these surfaces most directly, and Google has shipped agentic booking across Search, Maps and AI Mode.

Which one matters for high-intent questions?

ChatGPT, for best-of prompts. "Best beachfront resort in Clearwater for a family" is a ChatGPT question, and ChatGPT answers it with Google Maps citations at a rate no other platform matches. It also held to a brand's official page under fabricated pressure in Ahrefs' experiment, where five other products did not. Your own site earns ChatGPT citations when it states the facts directly.

Which one is hardest to move?

Claude, because its hospitality citation data is thin and its most-cited domain rests on a single source. I report Claude results and flag them as least stable. Perplexity and Gemini move fastest on off-site corroboration, because they retrieve live and lean on third parties.

What does the disagreement mean in practice?

The fix list differs per platform. Maps and your own page for ChatGPT. OTA listings and editorial lists for Perplexity. YouTube and the Business Profile for Google's surfaces. A report that averages six platforms into one score tells you nothing you can act on, and a vendor that offers one is selling you the average of six different weather systems.

Three things worth sitting with

For your business. Pick the two platforms your guests use. For most North American hotels that is Google's surfaces and ChatGPT. Measure each separately, three runs per prompt across two days. Read the cited sources per platform.

For your role. Somebody has to hold six fix lists and know which one moved. That is a new competency, and it does not live in the SEO agency.

For your guests. They do not know which engine they are using. All they know is they asked and got three names. Which engine decides which three.

Where this is going

OpenAI stepped back from direct purchases inside ChatGPT. Google stepped forward. That divergence tells you who is building the rails, and it tells you where the transaction will happen first.

Re-check quarterly. ChatGPT's Reddit citations fell six-fold in one month in 2025. The engines are not settling. They are specializing.
