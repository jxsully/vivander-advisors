Ten questions to ask before working with Vivander

What is Vivander Advisors, and what does AI-native mean?

Vivander Advisors is an AI-native company led by Michael J. Goldrich that helps businesses connect artificial intelligence to revenue, productivity and the way people work. AI is part of how Vivander researches, creates, builds and delivers. Experienced human leadership determines the business question, checks the evidence and takes responsibility for the work.

Vivander’s main offers connect three needs: understand what AI tells customers about your organization, help your organization’s employees use AI well, and lead the changes that turn experiments into useful operations. An AI visibility audit and monitoring provide evidence. Training develops skills. Fractional leadership connects strategy to rollout. Speaking, research and writing help organizations understand and explain the shift. Meet Michael and explore Vivander’s background.

Which organizations does Vivander support beyond hospitality?

Vivander works with business owners, leadership teams, marketing organizations and operating teams that need practical AI direction. Hospitality is an area of substantial experience, including hotels and businesses navigating changing customer discovery and direct-booking journeys. The approach also applies to agencies and organizations outside hospitality when the problem fits the scope.

What does an AI visibility audit provide beyond a dashboard?

Vivander Audit investigates what AI tells prospective buyers and what your organization’s team should examine next. It tests relevant buyer questions, distinguishes a mention from a citation or recommendation, checks business facts and examines website content and external sources. The deliverable connects the findings to a prioritized action plan with responsibilities.

A dashboard is useful for watching answers change. A diagnostic asks why a finding deserves attention and which work could address it. For example, an incorrect service claim may require a fact correction across several sources; absence from a comparison answer may call for clearer evidence of fit. The agreed audit scope defines coverage, deliverables and retesting. Implementation and ongoing monitoring are separate choices, and an audit cannot guarantee rankings, recommendations or sales.

How does Visibility Monitoring differ from an audit and from website analytics?

Vivander Visibility Monitoring tracks recurring buyer questions and retains answer evidence so your organization can examine changes in visibility, competitors and cited sources. An audit is a scoped investigation and action plan. Monitoring is an ongoing measurement cycle. Website analytics records visits and actions on your organization’s site; it does not show every conversation in which an assistant mentioned your organization without a click.

Use the three together. An audit establishes findings and priorities. Monitoring tests whether the answers change. Search Console and analytics show impressions, clicks, visits and tracked inquiries. Keep branded questions separate from questions that do not name your organization. Compare the same questions under a documented method, and record changes to platforms and prompts. A higher visibility score is a signal to inspect, rather than proof of commercial impact.

Can Vivander join your organization’s team as a fractional AI leader?

Yes. Fractional AI leadership places experienced support alongside your existing people for an agreed amount of time. Vivander can help shape the AI strategy, prioritize use cases, coordinate owners and partners, guide pilots, build staff capability and support rollout. Vivander can participate in your working cadence and connect leadership decisions to the team doing the work.

The role can combine strategic direction with practical delivery support. Your managers, IT team, marketing agency and subject experts remain important collaborators. The written scope defines the responsibilities, time commitment, meetings, access, decision rights and deliverables. Fractional leadership suits a company that needs continuity and ownership across several initiatives; a standalone workshop or audit suits a more focused need. Vivander agrees the model around your organization.

What would developing an AI strategy and rolling it out actually involve?

Vivander starts with business goals, current tools, recurring work and the people involved. The strategy turns those inputs into priorities: where AI can help, what should remain a human decision, which inputs are suitable, who owns each workflow and how success will be judged. A useful strategy includes a sequenced roadmap and operating decisions, rather than a list of tools.

Rollout then moves through focused pilots, quality checks, role-specific training, communication, documented review steps and staged expansion. Staff need a place to raise problems and managers need a way to judge progress. Depending on scope, outputs can include a use-case backlog, pilot briefs, a responsibility map, training plan and progress dashboard. See Vivander’s company AI rollout guide for an illustrative sequence. Timing depends on readiness and the work chosen.

How does Vivander develop AI fluency rather than just teach prompting?

Vivander training helps people choose suitable tasks, provide useful context, evaluate outputs and know when to ask for help or approval. Prompting is one skill within that larger set. The practical goal is better work that employees can repeat using tools the organization approves.

Programs can use Vivander’s GEAR methodology or a customized approach. Vivander agrees the starting point and learning outcomes, uses examples relevant to participants’ roles, and reinforces application through practice and review when that is part of the scope. A marketing exercise, for example, should test factual accuracy and brand fit alongside draft speed. A manager needs to evaluate the review burden and ownership. Vivander’s AI fluency measurement guide explains how to assess capability with real tasks.

How do personal AI agents, Sessions and MyTeam fit into Vivander’s work?

Personal AI agents can help an individual prepare research, organize information and follow a sequence of steps using connected tools. Their usefulness depends on the task, inputs, permissions, reliability and review process. Vivander’s advisory and training work can help a team evaluate those conditions and decide whether an agent is appropriate.

Vivander Sessions explores guided agent-building and learning. MyTeam explores daily commitments, coaching and AI teammate support. Both are smaller products in development and testing; discuss current availability before relying on them for a business rollout. A fractional engagement, a training program and a product subscription have different scopes. Vivander’s personal AI agents guide provides a practical way to choose a first workflow.

Can Vivander work with your organization’s existing tools, agency and IT team?

Yes. Vivander begins with the team, partners and tools your organization already uses. The aim is to connect responsibilities around the work: leadership agrees priorities, IT and relevant owners assess access and data handling, subject experts define quality, and the people using the workflow provide feedback. An existing agency can implement content or marketing changes from an agreed plan.

Vivander clarifies what Vivander will lead, prepare or support, and which decisions stay with your organization. Tool licenses, integration development and specialist implementation should be explicit in the scope. This avoids treating the presence of an AI tool as proof that the wider workflow is ready.

Visibility audit

What is a Vivander AI visibility audit?

Vivander Audit is a scoped diagnostic of how AI answers the questions your organization’s buyers ask. The audit combines platform testing with website, content and external-source checks, then turns the evidence into prioritized fixes.

What does your organization receive from the audit?

Vivander provides a visibility score and its underlying findings, a distinction between mentions, citations and recommendations, a fact-check record, and an action plan ranked by priority and tagged by who can make each change.

How long does Vivander Audit take?

The published product scope states two to three weeks from intake to delivery. Confirm timing when Vivander scopes your organization, buyer groups, locations and competitors.

Which AI platforms does the audit test?

The current Vivander Audit method tests buyer questions on seven AI platforms, with three attempts per question. Monitoring is a separate product with engine coverage determined by your organization’s plan and optional add-ons. See the current testing method on the product site.

Is an AI visibility audit different from an SEO audit?

An SEO audit examines how search engines can discover and rank pages. An AI visibility audit examines whether AI answers name, cite or recommend a business for buyer questions. Website access and content quality can matter to both, but the outcomes are different.

Can your organization’s existing agency make the fixes?

Yes. Vivander’s action plan identifies what needs attention and who can act. The audit does not automatically include implementation. Your organization’s team, agency and any additional Vivander support can work from the agreed findings.

How much does the audit cost?

Vivander quotes the audit after a short scoping conversation. The current product site does not publish a universal fixed price. Request a written scope for your organization.

What happens after the audit?

Retests at 60 and 90 days are available only if the client signing up for the audit wants them. They are optional, not automatic. Ongoing weekly monitoring and implementation support are separate choices to discuss when agreeing scope.

Visibility monitoring

What does Vivander Visibility Monitoring measure?

Vivander Monitoring records how AI answers your buyer questions and tracks visibility, competitors, sentiment, position and cited sources. The full saved answer remains available behind each reading.

How often are buyer questions tested?

The current product runs weekly cycles. Each core prompt is asked three times on each enabled engine; the plan and optional add-ons determine engine coverage.

What is the Live Visibility Score?

The Live Visibility Score combines seven weighted components over the last four completed cycles. The score is a measurement instrument, rather than a prediction of sales or a guarantee of recommendation.

Why does Vivander keep a fixed core of prompts?

A fixed, versioned set keeps the comparison meaningful. If the questions change, the ruler changes. Vivander marks changes to the core rather than presenting them as uninterrupted performance trends.

Are branded questions included in the score?

No. Vivander reports branded questions separately as brand recognition. Questions that already contain your organization’s name are easier to appear in and would otherwise inflate the headline visibility measurement.

Does monitoring replace an audit?

No. Monitoring measures recurring answers and changes over time. An audit examines causes across your organization’s website, content and external sources and gives your organization’s team a prioritized action plan.

Can monitoring use answers collected by another tool?

The monitoring product offers a bring-your-own-answer route. Check the current requirements and scope on its Bring Your Own page before choosing a plan.

Where are current monitoring prices listed?

Current plans, engine coverage and terms are on the Vivander Monitoring pricing page. Use that page for the latest offer rather than relying on a copied price.

Vivander MyTeam combines a daily accountability coach with AI teammates that prepare drafts, research and analysis. The coach compares your morning commitments with the recorded work at the end of the day.

AI scaling

What does it mean to scale an AI pilot?

Scaling means turning a useful experiment into a repeatable workflow with clear ownership, approved inputs, review steps and measures. Vivander helps teams decide whether the pilot is ready and what needs to change.

How should your organization choose a pilot to scale?

Start with a recurring task, a defined user and a result your organization can assess. Compare usefulness, frequency, data access, review burden and potential impact before choosing the pilot.

What should your organization measure?

Measure quality, completion time, adoption, exceptions and business outcomes relevant to the workflow. A faster draft is not a success if the review effort or error rate rises.

Does every task need an AI agent?

No. A clearer process, reusable prompt or existing tool may be sufficient. Vivander considers the simplest workable approach before adding autonomous steps or integrations.

Who owns the workflow after implementation?

Your organization should name a process owner and the people responsible for reviewing outputs, managing exceptions and maintaining instructions. Vivander can help define those responsibilities in scope.

Can your organization use existing tools?

Often, yes. Vivander begins with your existing tools, access permissions and constraints. New software is considered only when the workflow requires it.

How does Vivander manage human review?

The workflow should identify what needs approval, who approves it and what happens when information is missing or uncertain. Review rules are defined before expanding use.

Where should your organization start if employees need preparation?

Start with a training conversation or a scoped discussion of your AI strategy and rollout priorities. Agree which tasks matter, what support the team needs and how your organization will assess useful progress.
