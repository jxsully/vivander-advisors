How Hotels Can Prepare for the AI Assistant Revolution

Michael J. Goldrich · March 6, 2025

Winning the Direct Booking Battle on Alexa+ and Beyond

Vivander Advisors helps businesses understand how AI describes and recommends them. This guide connects AI discoverability with clear business facts, buyer questions and evidence you can inspect.

With Amazon’s launch of Alexa+, travel search and booking is shifting yet again. Major brands like Tripadvisor and Fodor’s are already integrating with AI-driven assistants, positioning themselves at the top of the funnel in travel research. This raises a critical question for luxury boutique hotels:

How can you ensure that your property appears in AI-generated travel recommendations and not just in results controlled by OTAs?

Voice assistants like Alexa+, Siri, and ChatGPT-powered tools are becoming new gatekeepers in the travel decision-making process. This is more than a consumer trend. It’s a battle for direct bookings on a new front. Hoteliers must proactively optimize their online presence to ensure AI-powered searches work in their favor rather than handing bookings to intermediaries.

Here’s how boutique hotel teams can leverage AI voice assistants internally to secure visibility and direct bookings:

1. Understand AI Assistants as the New Search Engine

In the past, hotels optimized for Google search rankings.

Now, they need to think about how AI filters and presents information to travelers.

  • AI assistants prioritize trusted data sources like Tripadvisor, Fodor’s, Google Reviews, and OTAs.
  • Direct brand websites may be overlooked if they aren’t optimized for AI interactions.
  • AI-generated recommendations focus on relevance, user behavior, and voice-friendly content.

✅ Actionable Strategy:

  • Ensure your hotel’s data is accurate and complete on platforms like Google Business Profile, Tripadvisor, and voice-activated metasearch platforms.
  • Track voice search trends (e.g., “Alexa, find a boutique hotel near me with ocean views”).

2. Optimize for Voice Search and Conversational AI

Unlike traditional SEO, voice search queries are longer and more conversational.

Instead of searching “luxury hotel in Miami,” a traveler might ask:

“Alexa, what’s the best boutique hotel in Miami with a rooftop pool and spa?”

AI will pull responses from structured data, user reviews, and rich, voice-optimized content.

✅ Actionable Strategy:

  • Rewrite website content in a Q&A format that aligns with how travelers speak rather than type.
  • Implement structured data (schema markup) for hotels to help AI assistants understand your offerings.
  • Ensure room descriptions and amenities are phrased in natural, spoken language for AI parsing.

3. Leverage AI-Powered Partnerships

Since Tripadvisor, Fodor’s, and Uber have already partnered with Alexa+, hotels must ensure they are active on these platforms.

✅ Actionable Strategy:

  • Maintain an optimized, up-to-date profile on Tripadvisor (including detailed responses to reviews).
  • Use Tripadvisor Plus or Sponsored Placements to boost your AI-generated ranking.
  • Partner with AI-integrated concierge platforms to offer frictionless bookings.

4. Use AI Assistants Internally to Enhance the Guest Experience

Hoteliers should not only compete for AI-driven bookings but also integrate AI tools within their own guest experience.

✅ Actionable Strategy:

  • Deploy AI-powered chatbots on your hotel’s website that mirror the way Alexa+ interacts with travelers.
  • Implement voice-activated concierge services in-room, allowing guests to:
    • Request room service
    • Book spa appointments
    • Get local dining recommendations
  • Train staff to use AI-powered insights from guest queries to improve offerings and personalize marketing.

5. The Future

Right now, Alexa+ doesn’t handle bookings directly but it’s only a matter of time before voice assistants integrate direct booking capabilities.

If hotels don’t prepare, OTAs will take the lead.

✅ Actionable Strategy:

  • Watch for future Alexa+ API integrations allowing direct hotel bookings.
  • Push for brand partnerships with AI platforms to ensure your property appears in native AI recommendations before OTAs.
  • Monitor AI-driven “conversational commerce” trends that could allow hotels to secure direct bookings through voice commands.

AI Assistants as the New Battleground for Direct Bookings

Hotels can no longer think of AI voice assistants as just a novelty. They are now a crucial part of the guest booking journey. The properties that optimize early will win direct bookings before OTAs monopolize AI-driven recommendations.

The future of travel search isn’t just about Google. It’s about making sure Alexa, Siri, and ChatGPT say your hotel’s name first.

Are you ready?
