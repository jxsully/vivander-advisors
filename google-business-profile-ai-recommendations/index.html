Does Google Business Profile affect what AI recommends?

Michael J. Goldrich · September 2026

Yes, and it reaches further than Google. ChatGPT cites Google Maps in best-of queries at roughly 188 citations per 1,000 queries, per Muck Rack's May 2026 data, so your profile feeds the highest-intent class of ChatGPT answers as well as Google's own. Google's entire official statement on the link between profiles and AI features is to keep the Business Profile and Merchant Center up to date. Short, and also the whole of it. At Vivander Advisors I treat the profile as the highest-priority channel for any business with a front door, on convergent evidence rather than on a percentage nobody can source.

I used to have that percentage. Then I withdrew it. More on that below.

The profile that promised a kids' club

A resort's Business Profile listed a spa and a kids' club. Its website mentioned neither in a heading.

Ask Maps answered "does this resort have a kids' club" with a hedge. ChatGPT left the property out of family prompts entirely. The profile said one thing. Its site said nothing. The engine split the difference against the hotel.

One heading on the website resolved both within the 30-day re-test.

Why the profile matters more than it did

Three changes in a year moved it to the top of the list.

Google discontinued the public Q&A on profiles from December 2025 and replaced it with Ask Maps, a Gemini-powered feature that answers a searcher's question in the moment from the profile's attributes, services, products, reviews, photos and the linked website. There is no single field to control any more. The whole profile is the answer. And Ask Maps cross-references the profile against the website and weakens credibility for any service claimed on one and absent from the other.

Google shipped agentic booking across Search, Maps and AI Mode. The reported mechanic: an agent calls the business's scheduling endpoint and awards the lead to whoever returns a real bookable slot fastest. A profile whose booking action ends in "call us" loses the lead before the phone rings.

The AI local pack contracted. Google's AI answers surface roughly a third of the businesses the classic three-pack did. A complete profile can fall out of the answer while staying on the map.

About that 79 percent

Earlier Vivander material carried a figure: 79.1 percent of hotel links in AI Mode go to Business Profile pages. In 2026 I traced it. No primary source, no date, no sample, no denominator.

Withdrawn. If you saw it in a deck of mine, stop using it. The profile's importance rests on the Muck Rack citation count, Google's own guidance, the Ask Maps mechanism and your own conversion path. That is enough, and it is honest, and I hold vendors to the same standard.

What on the profile does an assistant read?

Categories, primary and secondary. Attributes and service toggles. The products or services list. Photos, including whether they show recognizable exterior context, because the 2026 Maps update added spatial indexing and stock or generated imagery reads as an entity weakness. Reviews and owner responses. Hours. The booking or reservation action. And the linked website, which Ask Maps reads against all of it.

Why did my Map Pack visibility drop with a complete profile?

Check the AI local pack contraction first. You may be in the classic pack and out of the AI answer. Then agentic readiness: is there a fast, agent-callable booking action, or does the profile force a phone call. Last, the competitor teardown: which secondary categories and attribute toggles do the three winners carry that you do not, and what do their reviews praise that your copy never claims.

The fix is rarely "post more." It is closing specific category, attribute, action and corroboration gaps.

Three things worth sitting with

For your business. Make the profile and the website say the same things in the same words. Ask Maps checks, and it checks in real time.

For your role. The profile used to belong to whoever had the login. It is now a source that ChatGPT reads. That deserves an owner with a title.

For your guests. They will ask Maps a question and get an answer assembled from your profile and your site together. If those two disagree, the guest gets the hedge.

What I do not know

The share of AI recommendations for local businesses that trace to the profile rather than the website or a third party. I withdrew the only figure I had and have not replaced it with a guess.

Where this is going

Google confirmed at I/O 2026 that hotels are next for its universal commerce protocol, with an agent payment layer alongside. The search box, the recommendation, the price check, the payment, the confirmation. One conversation. None of it on your website.

The profile is the first thing that agent reads. It was a listing. Now it is becoming the front desk.
