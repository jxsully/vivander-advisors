What is the book Invisible: What To Do When AI Erases Your Business about?

Michael J. Goldrich · September 2026

Invisible: What To Do When AI Erases Your Business is my 2026 book about what happens to a business when customers ask AI where to go, what to buy and who to hire, and the business is not in the answer. It explains how AI assistants decide which businesses to name, cite and recommend, why a strong website, five-star reviews and years of SEO do not guarantee that AI knows your name, and gives a step-by-step system for showing up when AI answers the questions your customers ask. The five-band visibility ladder in every Vivander Advisors audit, from Invisible to Optimized, comes from the book.

I wrote it because I kept watching good businesses disappear from answers and nobody in the room could say why.

What is the book's central argument?

Discovery has moved from a search box to an answer, and the answer is built by machines from sources most businesses have never checked.

Millions of people skip the ten blue links and ask ChatGPT, Perplexity or a voice assistant for one recommendation. The assistant answers with three to five names, a sentence on each, and often no link. A business absent from that answer never existed for that customer. There is no page two, and no bid to raise.

I call it an existence problem, because "marketing problem" undersells it.

What does the book cover?

How assistants retrieve, select and cite. Why search rank and AI citation have separated. Which sources each platform trusts and why they disagree. The difference between being named, mentioned, cited and recommended. What has to be on a page for a machine to lift it. Why a few specific off-site sources can define a brand. How to measure visibility honestly, with repeated runs and a published method. And a sequence of fixes, cheapest first, that a business can run with an editor before it spends a dollar on anything else.

It is essentially an up-to-date standard operating procedure for getting found by machines. I update the method as the evidence moves, and I say so.

Who is it for?

Owners, marketers and leaders of businesses that customers find by asking. Hotels, restaurants, retailers, spas, attractions, professional services. Hospitality is the proving ground because I spent 25 years running hotel commercial functions. The method applies to any business with a name, a place and a category.

How does it relate to Vivander's work?

The book is the method. Vivander's AI Visibility Audit is the method applied to one business: 50 or more prompts across six platforms, a fact ledger, a source list, re-tests. Answer Engine Content is the fix chapters delivered as a service. Most of my keynotes since 2026, including The Traffic That Disappeared at HSMAI, are built on it.

Where can I get it?

On Amazon in paperback and Kindle. Bulk orders for teams and conference audiences are available through Vivander Advisors.

Three times as many people reached out after my talks the week it launched. I would love to tell you it was me. The truth is simpler. Things are changing so fast that people are overwhelmed, and they are looking for anyone who can make it clear. That is the work.
